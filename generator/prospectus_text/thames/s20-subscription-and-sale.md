# 20. Subscription and sale

## The subscription agreement

Corvane Securities plc (the "Lead Manager") has, under a subscription agreement dated on or about the date of
this Prospectus between the Issuer, the Originator and the Lead Manager (the "Subscription Agreement"), agreed
with the Issuer, subject to the satisfaction of the conditions set out in it, to subscribe or procure
subscribers for the Notes on the Closing Date at an issue price of 100% of their initial principal amount. The
Originator has agreed in the Subscription Agreement to subscribe for 5% of the initial principal amount of each
class of Notes in its capacity as Retention Holder, as described in section 18, and the Lead Manager's
obligation applies to the remaining Notes of each class.

The obligations of the Lead Manager under the Subscription Agreement are subject to conditions precedent that
include: (a) the execution and delivery of the Transaction Documents by each party to them on or before the
Closing Date; (b) the delivery of legal opinions from Hartwell Quinn LLP and Grantley Moss LLP and of
certificates from the directors of the Issuer and of the Originator, in a form satisfactory to the Lead
Manager; (c) the assignment of the ratings described in section 4 to the Notes by the Rating Agencies, and the
absence of any downgrade or announcement of a possible downgrade before the Closing Date; (d) the accuracy of
the representations and warranties of the Issuer and the Originator on the date of the Subscription Agreement
and on the Closing Date; and (e) the absence, before the Closing Date, of any material adverse change in the
financial condition or prospects of the Issuer or the Originator, or of any change in national or
international financial, political or economic conditions which in the opinion of the Lead Manager would be
likely to prejudice materially the success of the offering of the Notes.

The Issuer and the Originator have given representations and warranties to the Lead Manager about themselves,
the Loans, the Transaction Documents and the information contained in this Prospectus. The Originator has
agreed to indemnify the Lead Manager against certain liabilities incurred in connection with the issue of the
Notes, and to pay the fees, commissions and expenses of the Arranger and the Lead Manager and the other costs
of the transaction, as described in section 6. The Lead Manager is entitled to terminate the Subscription
Agreement in certain circumstances before payment for the Notes is made to the Issuer, including a breach of
warranty, the failure of a condition precedent or a material change in market conditions.

Settlement of the Notes will take place on the Closing Date on a delivery-versus-payment basis through the
Clearing Systems, as described in section 21. The Issuer will not receive any proceeds from the Notes until
the Closing Date, and it will apply the proceeds as described in section 6.

## Selling restrictions: general

The Lead Manager has represented, warranted and agreed that it has complied and will comply with all
applicable laws and regulations in each country or jurisdiction in or from which it purchases, offers, sells
or delivers Notes, or possesses, distributes or publishes this Prospectus or any other offering material
relating to the Notes, in all cases at its own expense. The Lead Manager will obtain any consent, approval or
permission required by it for the purchase, offer, sale or delivery of the Notes under the laws and
regulations in force in any jurisdiction to which it is subject or in which it makes such purchases, offers,
sales or deliveries.

No action has been or will be taken by the Issuer, the Originator, the Arranger or the Lead Manager that
would, or is intended to, permit a public offer of the Notes, or the possession, circulation or distribution
of this Prospectus or of any other material relating to the Issuer, the Originator or the Notes, in any
country or jurisdiction where action for that purpose is required. Accordingly, the Notes may not be offered
or sold, directly or indirectly, and neither this Prospectus nor any other offering material or advertisement
in connection with the Notes may be distributed or published, in or from any country or jurisdiction except in
circumstances that will result in compliance with all applicable laws and regulations.

## United Kingdom

The Lead Manager has represented, warranted and agreed that:

(a) *Financial promotion*: it has only communicated or caused to be communicated, and will only communicate or
cause to be communicated, an invitation or inducement to engage in investment activity (within the meaning of
section 21 of FSMA) received by it in connection with the issue or sale of any Notes in circumstances in
which section 21(1) of FSMA does not apply to the Issuer;

(b) *General compliance*: it has complied and will comply with all applicable provisions of FSMA and of
the rules and regulations made under it with respect to anything done by it in relation to the Notes in, from
or otherwise involving the United Kingdom;

(c) *Public offers*: it has not made and will not make an offer of the Notes to the public in the United
Kingdom, other than an offer that falls within an exception set out in Schedule 1 to the Public Offers and
Admissions to Trading Regulations 2024, including an offer made only to qualified investors, and no Notes have
been or will be offered in circumstances which would require the Issuer or the Lead Manager to publish a
prospectus or any other offering document under those regulations;

(d) *Retail investors*: it has not offered, sold or otherwise made available, and will not offer, sell or
otherwise make available, any Notes to any retail investor in the United Kingdom, as defined in the notice
headed *Prohibition of sales to UK retail investors* in the Important notices of this Prospectus; and

(e) *Securitisation positions*: it will not sell any Note to a retail client in the United Kingdom in
contravention of the restrictions on the sale of securitisation positions to retail clients in the
Securitisation Regulations 2024 and SECN.

This Prospectus is directed only at persons in the United Kingdom who (i) have professional experience in
matters relating to investments falling within Article 19(5) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000
(Financial Promotion) Order 2005 (the "Financial Promotion Order"), (ii) are high net worth companies,
unincorporated associations and other persons falling within Article 49(2)(a) to (d) of the Financial
Promotion Order, or (iii) are persons to whom it may otherwise lawfully be communicated (all such persons
together, "relevant persons"). This Prospectus must not be acted on or relied on by persons who are not
relevant persons. Any investment or investment activity to which this Prospectus relates is available only to
relevant persons and will be engaged in only with relevant persons.

## European Economic Area

In relation to each member state of the EEA, the Lead Manager has represented, warranted and agreed that:

(a) *Public offers*: it has not made and will not make an offer of Notes to the public in that member state,
except an offer of Notes to any legal entity which is a qualified investor as defined in Regulation (EU)
2017/1129 (as amended, the "EU Prospectus Regulation"), or in any other circumstances falling within Article
1(4) of the EU Prospectus Regulation, provided that no such offer requires the Issuer or the Lead Manager to
publish a prospectus under Article 3 of the EU Prospectus Regulation or to supplement a prospectus under
Article 23 of the EU Prospectus Regulation;

(b) *Retail investors*: it has not offered, sold or otherwise made available, and will not offer, sell or
otherwise make available, any Notes to any retail investor in the EEA, as defined in the notice headed
*Prohibition of sales to EEA retail investors* in the Important notices of this Prospectus; and

(c) *Securitisation positions*: it will not sell any Note to a retail client in the EEA except in compliance
with Article 3 of the EU Securitisation Regulation.

For these purposes, an "offer of Notes to the public" means the communication in any form and by any means of
sufficient information on the terms of the offer and the Notes to be offered so as to enable an investor to
decide to purchase or subscribe for the Notes.

EEA investors should note that the transaction has been structured to comply with the UK securitisation
framework. Each EEA institutional investor is responsible for its own assessment of whether the information
made available by the Issuer and the Retention Holder, described in sections 18 and 21, is sufficient to
enable it to satisfy the due diligence requirements of Article 5 of the EU Securitisation Regulation. None of
the Issuer, the Originator, the Arranger and the Lead Manager makes any representation that it is.

## United States

The Notes have not been and will not be registered under the Securities Act or the securities laws of any
state or other jurisdiction of the United States, and they may not be offered, sold or delivered within the
United States or to, or for the account or benefit of, U.S. persons (as defined in Regulation S under the
Securities Act ("Regulation S")), except in certain transactions exempt from, or not subject to, the
registration requirements of the Securities Act. The Lead Manager has represented, warranted and agreed that:

(a) *Offshore transactions*: it has offered and sold, and will offer and sell, the Notes only outside the
United States in offshore transactions to persons who are not U.S. persons, in reliance on Regulation S, both
as part of its distribution at any time and otherwise until 40 days after the later of the commencement of the
offering and the Closing Date (the "distribution compliance period");

(b) *No directed selling efforts*: neither it, nor its affiliates, nor any person acting on its or their
behalf has engaged or will engage in any directed selling efforts with respect to the Notes, and it and they
have complied and will comply with the offering restrictions requirement of Regulation S;

(c) *Confirmations*: it will have sent to each distributor, dealer or other person to which it sells Notes
during the distribution compliance period a confirmation or other notice setting out the restrictions on
offers and sales of the Notes within the United States or to, or for the account or benefit of, U.S. persons;
and

(d) *Risk retention*: it will not sell Notes to, or for the account or benefit of, any person that is a "U.S.
person" as defined in the credit risk retention regulations issued under Section 15G of the U.S. Securities
Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the "U.S. Risk Retention Rules"), without the prior written consent of the
Originator.

The transaction is not intended to involve the retention by a sponsor of a credit risk under the U.S. Risk
Retention Rules. The Issuer intends to rely on the exemption for non-U.S. transactions in Section 20 of the
U.S. Risk Retention Rules, which requires, among other conditions, that no more than 10% of the dollar value of
all classes of interests issued in the securitisation be sold or transferred to, or for the account or benefit
of, persons that are U.S. persons for the purposes of those rules. Each purchaser of Notes will be deemed to
represent that it is not such a U.S. person, or that it has obtained the consent of the Originator.

The Issuer has not been and will not be registered under the U.S. Investment Company Act of 1940, as amended.
Terms used in this paragraph and not otherwise defined have the meanings given to them by Regulation S.

## Prohibition of sales to retail investors

For the avoidance of doubt, the Notes are offered only to professional investors and eligible counterparties.
No Notes may be offered, sold or otherwise made available to any retail investor in the United Kingdom or the
EEA, whether in the initial distribution or in any subsequent resale, and no key information document has been
prepared for the Notes under the UK or EEA rules described in the Important notices. Each purchaser of Notes
will be deemed to represent and agree that it is not a retail investor in the United Kingdom or the EEA and
that it will not offer, sell or otherwise make available any Note to such a person. Any Distributor that
resells the Notes is responsible for its own compliance with the product governance and retail distribution
rules that apply to it.
