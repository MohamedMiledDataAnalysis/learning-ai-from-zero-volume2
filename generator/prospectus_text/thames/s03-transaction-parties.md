# 3. Transaction parties

The table below lists each party to the securitisation of motor finance receivables described in this
Prospectus (the "Transaction") and the role it performs. Each party is described in more detail in the
paragraphs that follow. The documents under which each party acts (together, the "Transaction Documents") are
listed in section 21, where they are also stated to be available for inspection.

In this Prospectus, a "Loan" means each hire purchase or personal contract purchase agreement sold by the Seller
to the Issuer on the Closing Date, together with its related rights, and the "Portfolio" means all the Loans
from time to time; a "Borrower" means the individual who is the customer under a Loan. A "Collection Period"
means each calendar month, the first Collection Period being January 2026 (period 1), and a "Payment Date"
means the [[payment_day]] day of each month, the first Payment Date being [[first_payment_date]]; each
Collection Period is followed by the Payment Date in the next month. An "Insolvency Event" occurs in respect
of a company if it is unable or admits its inability to pay its debts as they fall due within the meaning of
section 123 of the Insolvency Act 1986, if an administrator, liquidator, receiver or similar officer is
appointed over it or over the whole or a substantial part of its assets, if it enters into a voluntary
arrangement or other composition with its creditors generally, or if an order is made or an effective
resolution is passed for its winding-up (other than a solvent reorganisation approved by the Trustee). The
holders of the Notes are the "Noteholders", as defined in the Important notices.

## Thames Auto Receivables 2026-1 plc

Thames Auto Receivables 2026-1 plc (the "Issuer") is a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales.
It is a special purpose vehicle established for the sole purpose of issuing the Notes, purchasing the Loans
from the Seller and entering into the Transaction Documents. The Issuer has no employees and no subsidiaries,
and its activities are restricted by its articles of association and by the covenants in the trust deed
constituting the Notes (the "Trust Deed"). Its share capital, directors, capitalisation and accounts are
described in section 7.

## Holdings

Thames Auto Receivables Holdings Ltd ("Holdings") is a private limited company incorporated in England and
Wales. Holdings holds the entire issued share capital of the Issuer. The entire issued share capital of
Holdings is held by the Share Trustee on a discretionary trust for charitable purposes. Neither the Originator
nor any company in its group owns, directly or indirectly, any share in Holdings or the Issuer, and neither the
Issuer nor Holdings is controlled by the Originator. Holdings is described in section 7.

## Kestrel Motor Finance Ltd

Kestrel Motor Finance Ltd (the "Originator") is a private limited company incorporated in England and Wales and
authorised and regulated by the FCA to carry on consumer credit lending and related regulated activities. It
provides hire purchase and personal contract purchase finance for new and used cars to individuals throughout
the United Kingdom, through a network of franchised and independent motor dealers. The Originator originated
each of the Loans in the portfolio.

Under the loan sale agreement dated on or about the Closing Date (the "Sale Agreement"), Kestrel Motor
Finance Ltd (the "Seller") will sell the Loans and their related rights to the Issuer
on the Closing Date. The Seller gives the loan warranties described in section 12 and is obliged to repurchase
any loan that breaches a loan warranty in the circumstances described in that section.

Under the servicing agreement dated on or about the Closing Date (the "Servicing Agreement"), Kestrel Motor
Finance Ltd (the "Servicer") will continue to administer the Loans on behalf of the
Issuer, collect payments from Borrowers, manage arrears, terminate agreements and repossess and sell vehicles
in accordance with its policies and procedures, and prepare the information on the Loans that the Cash Manager
uses for the investor reports. The Servicer's duties, its fee and the circumstances in which it may be
replaced are described in section 13.

The Originator will also act as the retention holder for the purposes of the UK securitisation framework (the
"Retention Holder"): it will retain on an ongoing basis a material net economic interest in the securitisation
of not less than 5% of the nominal value of each class of Notes (a vertical slice), as described in section 18.
Kestrel Motor Finance Ltd was incorporated in England and Wales on 12 March 2004 with registered number
05087341, and its head office is in Leicester. The Transaction is its first public securitisation; certain of
the Loans are financed, until the Closing Date, under a private warehouse facility provided to the Originator
by a United Kingdom bank and are released from that facility on the Closing Date (see section 6).
The Originator is a wholly owned subsidiary of Kestrel Group plc, a public limited company incorporated in
England and Wales. Kestrel Group plc does not guarantee any obligation of the Originator under the Transaction
Documents. The Originator, its history and its business are described in section 8.

## The Trustee

The Trustee will act as note trustee for the Noteholders under the Trust Deed and as security trustee for the
Secured Creditors under the deed of charge dated on or about the Closing Date (the "Deed of Charge"). Under the
Deed of Charge the Issuer grants to the Trustee, for the benefit of the Secured Creditors, an assignment by way
of security of its rights in the Loans and the Transaction Documents and a first-ranking charge over the
Issuer Accounts and its other assets. The Trustee is a trust corporation regulated in the United Kingdom. Its
fees and expenses are paid by the Issuer as a senior fee in the Priority of Payments (section 15). The Trustee
has no obligation to monitor the performance of the Loans or the compliance of any party with the Transaction
Documents, and it is entitled to be indemnified and secured to its satisfaction before it takes any action.
The rights of the Trustee and the circumstances in which it may be removed or may retire are set out in the
Conditions (section 5).

## Larkmead Bank plc

Larkmead Bank plc (the "Cash Manager") is a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales and
authorised by the Prudential Regulation Authority (the "PRA") and regulated by the FCA and the PRA. Under the
cash management agreement dated on or about the Closing Date (the "Cash Management Agreement"), the Cash
Manager will operate the Issuer Accounts, calculate the Available Funds and the amounts payable on each
Payment Date, apply the Available Funds in accordance with the Priority of Payments, maintain the ledgers of
the Issuer and prepare the investor reports from the information provided by the Servicer.

Larkmead Bank plc (the "Account Bank") will hold the transaction account and the reserve account of the
Issuer (together, the "Issuer Accounts") under the bank account agreement dated on or about the Closing Date
(the "Bank Account Agreement"). If the Account Bank ceases to have the minimum ratings required by the
Rating Agencies, it must, within the period set out in the Bank Account Agreement, transfer the Issuer Accounts
to a suitably rated bank or obtain a guarantee from a suitably rated guarantor, as described in section 14.

Larkmead Bank plc (the "Collection Account Bank") also holds the account of the Servicer into which Borrowers
pay their instalments (the "Collection Account"). The Collection Account is held on trust for the Issuer and
the other persons entitled to the collections credited to it, and collections on the Loans are swept to the
transaction account of the Issuer, as described in sections 13 and 14.

Larkmead Bank plc (the "Agent Bank") will determine the rate of interest on each class of Notes for each
interest period under the agency agreement dated on or about the Closing Date (the "Agency Agreement"), and
Larkmead Bank plc (the "Principal Paying Agent") will make payments of interest and principal on the Notes to
the Clearing Systems for credit to the accounts of the Noteholders. The fees of Larkmead Bank plc in each of
its capacities, and the fee of the Back-up Servicer Facilitator, are paid by Kestrel Motor Finance Ltd out of
the servicer fee it receives under the Servicing Agreement, and not by the Issuer.

## Pellam Corporate Services Ltd

Pellam Corporate Services Ltd (the "Corporate Services Provider") is a private limited company incorporated in
England and Wales that provides directors, company secretarial, accounting and administrative services to
special purpose companies. Under the corporate services agreement dated on or about the Closing Date (the
"Corporate Services Agreement"), it will provide the directors and the company secretary of the Issuer and of
Holdings, keep their statutory books and arrange the preparation and filing of their accounts and returns.

Pellam Corporate Services Ltd (the "Share Trustee") holds the entire issued share capital of Holdings on a
discretionary trust for charitable purposes under a declaration of trust (the "Share Trust Deed"), as
described in section 7.

Pellam Corporate Services Ltd (the "Back-up Servicer Facilitator") will, under the Servicing Agreement, use
reasonable endeavours to identify and assist the Issuer and the Trustee in appointing a substitute servicer if
the appointment of the Servicer is terminated following a servicer termination event, as described in section
13. The Back-up Servicer Facilitator does not itself undertake to service the Loans.

## Corvane Securities plc

Corvane Securities plc (the "Arranger") is a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales and
authorised and regulated by the FCA. It structured the transaction. Corvane Securities plc (the "Lead
Manager") has agreed, subject to the conditions in the subscription agreement described in section 20, to
subscribe or procure subscribers for the Notes that the Retention Holder does not acquire.

## Legal advisers and auditors

Grantley Moss LLP, a limited liability partnership registered in England and Wales, is the legal adviser to the
Arranger, the Lead Manager and the Trustee as to English law. Hartwell Quinn LLP, a limited liability
partnership registered in England and Wales, is the legal adviser to the Issuer and the Originator as to
English law and as to the United Kingdom tax treatment of the Issuer. Fennick & Rowe LLP, a limited liability
partnership registered in England and Wales and a member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England
and Wales, has been appointed as the auditors of the Issuer and of Holdings (the "Auditors").

## The Rating Agencies

The Notes are expected to be rated by the Rating Agencies on the Closing Date, as set out in section 4. Each of
the Rating Agencies is established in the United Kingdom and registered under the UK CRA Regulation. The
Rating Agencies are not parties to the Transaction Documents. Certain actions of the parties, including the
replacement of the Account Bank and certain amendments to the Transaction Documents, are subject to
confirmation that the then current ratings of the Notes will not be downgraded, withdrawn or qualified as a
result.

## Conflicts of interest

Each of the parties, and its affiliates, may engage in other business with the Originator, the Issuer or
other parties and may act in more than one capacity in this transaction. In particular, Kestrel Motor Finance Ltd acts
in four capacities (originator, seller, servicer and retention holder), Larkmead Bank plc acts in five
capacities and Pellam Corporate Services Ltd acts in three capacities. In each capacity a party acts only in accordance with the Transaction
Documents to which it is a party in that capacity, and it may have interests that differ from those of the
Noteholders. The risks arising from these arrangements are described in section 2.
