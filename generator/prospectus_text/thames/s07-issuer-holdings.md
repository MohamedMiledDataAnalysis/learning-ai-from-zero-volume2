# 7. The issuer and Holdings

## Incorporation and status

Thames Auto Receivables 2026-1 plc (the "Issuer") was incorporated in England and Wales on 4 November 2025 as a
public company limited by shares under the Companies Act 2006, with registered number 16048213. The registered
office of the Issuer is at 8 Aldermary Lane, London EC4, and that is also its principal place of business. The
Issuer has no telephone number of its own; it may be contacted through the Corporate Services Provider at the
same address.

The Issuer was established as a special purpose vehicle for the purpose of issuing asset-backed securities. It
is an orphan company: it is not a subsidiary of the Originator or of any company in the Kestrel group, and no
party to the Transaction Documents other than Holdings has any ownership interest in it. The Issuer is a
"securitisation company" for the purposes of the Taxation of Securitisation Companies Regulations 2006 (SI
2006/3296), as described in section 19.

## Share capital of the Issuer

The issued share capital of the Issuer is £50,000, divided into 50,000 ordinary shares of £1 each. One share is
fully paid up and 49,999 shares are each paid up as to 25 pence, so that £12,500.75 of the issued share
capital has been paid up. Holdings is the legal and beneficial owner of 49,999 shares, and Pellam Corporate
Services Ltd holds the remaining share as nominee for Holdings under a declaration of trust in favour of
Holdings. The Issuer has no other class of shares, no shares held in treasury and no outstanding options,
warrants or rights to subscribe for shares, and it has not issued any convertible or exchangeable securities.

The rights of the shareholders are set out in the articles of association of the Issuer. Holdings may not
dispose of, or create any security interest over, the shares in the Issuer except in accordance with the
Transaction Documents.

## Principal activities of the Issuer

The principal objects of the Issuer are set out in its articles of association and include the purchase of
receivables, the issue of debt securities, the entry into financial arrangements in connection with them and
the grant of security for its obligations. Since its incorporation the Issuer has not engaged in any material
activities other than those incidental to its incorporation, to its registration as a public company under the
Companies Act 2006, to the authorisation and issue of the Notes, to the matters described or contemplated in
this Prospectus and to the authorisation, execution, delivery and performance of the Transaction Documents. The
Issuer has not commenced operations and has not prepared any financial statements as at the date of this
Prospectus.

From the Closing Date the Issuer's activities will consist of holding the Loans and their related rights,
receiving collections on them through the Servicer, paying interest and principal on the Notes and its other
obligations in the order set out in the Priority of Payments in section 15, and performing its obligations and
exercising its rights under the Transaction Documents. All of its assets will be charged to the Trustee under
the Deed of Charge to secure its obligations to the Secured Creditors.

## Restrictions on the Issuer

So long as any Note is outstanding, the Issuer has covenanted with the Trustee in the Trust Deed that, save as
permitted by the Transaction Documents or with the prior written consent of the Trustee, it will not:

(a) carry on any business other than as described in this Prospectus and as contemplated by the Transaction
Documents;

(b) incur any indebtedness for borrowed money, or give any guarantee or indemnity in respect of the
indebtedness of any person, other than the Notes and the obligations it assumes under the Transaction
Documents;

(c) create or permit to subsist any mortgage, charge, pledge, lien or other security interest over any of its
assets or its undertaking, other than the security created by the Deed of Charge;

(d) sell, transfer or otherwise dispose of any of its assets, other than in accordance with the Transaction
Documents;

(e) pay any dividend or make any other distribution to its shareholders, or issue any further shares;

(f) have any employees, own or lease any premises, or have any subsidiary undertaking;

(g) consolidate or merge with any other person or convey or transfer all or substantially all of its
properties or assets to any other person;

(h) open or have an interest in any bank account other than the Issuer Accounts, unless the account is charged
to the Trustee under the Deed of Charge;

(i) amend, supplement or waive any provision of its articles of association or of any Transaction Document,
except as provided in the Conditions (section 5); or

(j) take any action which would cause it to cease to be a securitisation company for the purposes of the
Taxation of Securitisation Companies Regulations 2006, or become resident for tax purposes in any jurisdiction
other than the United Kingdom.

## Holdings

Thames Auto Receivables Holdings Ltd ("Holdings") was incorporated in England and Wales on 3 November 2025 as a
private company limited by shares under the Companies Act 2006, with registered number 16047795. Its
registered office is at 8 Aldermary Lane, London EC4. The issued share capital of Holdings is £1, consisting of
one ordinary share of £1, which is fully paid up and held by the Share Trustee.

The principal objects of Holdings are to acquire and hold, by way of investment, the shares in the Issuer and
to carry on any business incidental to that purpose. Holdings has not engaged in any activities since its
incorporation other than those incidental to its incorporation, to the acquisition of the shares in the Issuer
and to the matters contemplated in this Prospectus. Holdings has no employees and no subsidiary other than the
Issuer, and it has given covenants to the Trustee, in a deed dated on or about the Closing Date, that it will
not dispose of or charge the shares of the Issuer, or permit the Issuer to issue further shares, while any
Note is outstanding. The directors of Holdings are the same persons as the directors of the Issuer, and its
company secretary is Pellam Corporate Services Ltd.

Holdings has no right to any payment from the Issuer under the Transaction Documents and receives no dividend
from the Issuer. It is not a party to the Deed of Charge and does not guarantee any obligation of the Issuer.

## The share trust

The Share Trustee holds the share in Holdings under the terms of the Share Trust Deed on a discretionary trust
for the benefit of one or more charities or for charitable purposes. The Share Trustee has no beneficial
interest in, and derives no benefit (other than its fees for acting as share trustee) from, its holding of the
share in Holdings. Any income it receives under the trust is applied for the benefit of the charitable
purposes in its discretion. The Share Trust Deed provides that the trust will be wound up, and the shares of
Holdings disposed of, only after all amounts due under the Notes and the other Transaction Documents have been
paid in full.

This structure is intended to ensure that neither the Issuer nor Holdings is a subsidiary undertaking of the
Originator or of any other party to the transaction for the purposes of the Companies Act 2006, and that the
assets of the Issuer are not consolidated with those of the Originator on the insolvency of the Originator. The
Originator does not control, directly or indirectly, the Issuer, Holdings or the Share Trustee, and none of
them is accustomed to act in accordance with the directions of the Originator.

## No employees and no subsidiaries

Neither the Issuer nor Holdings has, or will have while any Note is outstanding, any employees. The
administrative functions of the Issuer are performed by the Corporate Services Provider, the servicing of the
Loans by the Servicer and the operation of its bank accounts and the calculation of payments by the Cash
Manager, in each case under the relevant Transaction Document. The Issuer has no subsidiary undertakings and,
other than the Issuer, Holdings has none.

## Accounts and auditors

The accounting reference date of the Issuer and of Holdings is 31 December. The first statutory accounts of
the Issuer will be prepared for the period from its incorporation to 31 December 2026 and, in accordance with
the Companies Act 2006, will be filed at Companies House within six months of that date, as a public company.
The Issuer will prepare its accounts in accordance with United Kingdom generally accepted accounting practice,
including Financial Reporting Standard 102. Holdings will prepare its accounts in the same way and, as a
private company, will file them within nine months of its accounting reference date. Neither the Issuer nor
Holdings has prepared any financial statements or published any accounts as at the date of this Prospectus.

Fennick & Rowe LLP, chartered accountants and registered auditors, of London, have been appointed as the
auditors of the Issuer and of Holdings. Fennick & Rowe LLP is registered to carry out audit work in the United
Kingdom by the Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales. The Auditors have no material interest
in the Issuer or in Holdings.

Copies of the audited annual accounts of the Issuer, when published, will be available for inspection as
described in section 21, and the Issuer will deliver a copy of them to the Trustee and to the Principal Paying
Agent. The Issuer is not required by the Conditions to prepare interim accounts.
