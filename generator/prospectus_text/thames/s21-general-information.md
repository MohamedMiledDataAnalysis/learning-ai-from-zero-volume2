# 21. General information

## Authorisation

The issue of the Notes, and the entry by the Issuer into the Transaction Documents to which it is a party, were
authorised by resolutions of the board of directors of the Issuer passed on 19 January 2026. The Issuer has
obtained all necessary consents, approvals and authorisations in the United Kingdom in connection with the
issue and performance of the Notes. The entry by Holdings into the deed of covenant described in section 7 was
authorised by a resolution of its board of directors passed on the same date. The entry by the Originator into
the Transaction Documents to which it is a party was authorised by a resolution of its board of directors.

## Litigation

The Issuer is not, and has not been since its incorporation, involved in any governmental, legal or arbitration
proceedings (including any such proceedings which are pending or threatened of which the Issuer is aware) which
may have, or have had, a significant effect on the financial position or profitability of the Issuer. The
same statement is made by Holdings in respect of itself.

The Originator is, in the ordinary course of its business, party to complaints and claims by its customers,
including complaints referred to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and is affected by the review of
historical motor finance commission arrangements carried out by the FCA. The effect of these matters on the
Loans and the Notes, and the risks they present, are described in sections 2 and 8.

## No significant change

Since the date of its incorporation, there has been no material adverse change in the financial position or
prospects of the Issuer and no significant change in its financial or trading position. Since the date of its
incorporation, the Issuer has not entered into any contract or arrangement, other than in the ordinary course
of its business, and has not engaged in any activity, other than as described in section 7.

## Accounts

No statutory accounts have been prepared for the Issuer or for Holdings as at the date of this Prospectus. The
first financial period of each of them will end on 31 December 2026. The Issuer will publish its audited
annual accounts within the period required by the Companies Act 2006 and will deliver copies of them to the
Trustee and to the Principal Paying Agent. Fennick & Rowe LLP have been appointed as the Auditors, as described
in section 7. The Issuer does not intend to publish interim accounts.

note registered in the name of a nominee of a common safekeeper for the Clearing Systems. Definitive Notes will
be issued only in the limited circumstances set out in the Conditions (section 5). The identification codes of

## Investor reporting

The Cash Manager will prepare, on behalf of the Issuer, a monthly investor report for each period, using the
information on the Loans provided by the Servicer. The content of the investor report, the date by which it is
published and the tests it reports are described in section 16. The investor report will be made available to
the Noteholders, the Trustee, the Rating Agencies and, on request, to potential investors in the Notes.

## Post-issuance reporting under the UK securitisation framework

The Issuer has been designated, in accordance with the Securitisation Regulations 2024 and SECN 6 of the FCA
Handbook, as the entity responsible for fulfilling the transparency requirements of the UK securitisation
framework (the "Reporting Entity"), and the Originator has agreed to provide it with all the information it
needs to do so. The Reporting Entity will make the following information available to the holders of the
Notes, the competent authorities and, on request, potential investors, by means of a securitisation
repository or, if none is available, a website that meets the requirements of SECN 6:

(a) before pricing and on the Closing Date, the Transaction Documents, this Prospectus and the loan-level
information on the Loans, in each case in the form required by SECN 6;

(b) on a monthly basis, loan-level information on the Loans in the template required by SECN 6, published at
the same time as the investor report;

(c) on a monthly basis, an investor report in the form required by SECN 6, covering the performance of the
Loans, the cash flows and the application of funds, the trigger events, and the Retention Holder's
confirmation of its continued holding of the vertical slice described in section 18; and

(d) without delay, any significant event affecting the transaction, including a
material breach of the Transaction Documents, a change in the structural features or the risk characteristics
of the transaction, the occurrence of a trigger event, the termination of the appointment of the Servicer, and
any material amendment to the Transaction Documents.

The Issuer will publish each item of information described above within the periods set by SECN 6 and will
keep it available for at least five years after the final maturity date of the Notes ([[final_maturity_date]]).
The risk retention commitment of the Retention Holder and the other regulatory disclosures that apply to the
transaction are described in section 18.

## Interests of persons involved in the issue

Save for the fees payable to the Arranger and the Lead Manager, the fees payable to the transaction parties
under the Transaction Documents, the Deferred Consideration payable to the Seller and the Notes held by the
Originator as Retention Holder, so far as the Issuer is aware no person involved in the issue of the Notes has
an interest material to the offer. The capacities in which each party acts, and the conflicts of interest
which may arise from them, are described in sections 2 and 3.

## Expenses of the issue

The expenses incurred in connection with the issue of the Notes, including the fees of the Arranger and the
Lead Manager, the legal advisers, the Auditors and the Rating Agencies, will be paid by the Originator and not
by the Issuer, as described in section 6. The servicer fee and the trustee fee are paid by the Issuer as
senior fees in the Priority of Payments in section 15; the fees of the Cash Manager, the Account Bank,
the Collection Account Bank, the Agent Bank, the Principal Paying Agent and the Back-up Servicer Facilitator
are paid by the Servicer out of the servicer fee.

United Kingdom motor finance market and on used vehicle prices in sections 2 and 8, the Issuer confirms that
the information has been accurately reproduced and that, as far as the Issuer is aware and is able to
ascertain from information published by that third party, no facts have been omitted which would render the
reproduced information inaccurate or misleading. The Issuer has not independently verified any such
information.

The Notes, the Conditions and the Transaction Documents, and any non-contractual obligations arising out of or
in connection with them, are governed by English law, save as stated above for the Scottish Declaration of
Trust. The courts of England have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any dispute arising out of or in connection
with the Notes.
