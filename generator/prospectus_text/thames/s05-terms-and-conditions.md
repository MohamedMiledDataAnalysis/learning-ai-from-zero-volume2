completion and amendment, will be endorsed on each Note in definitive form (if issued) and will be attached to
and (subject to the provisions thereof) apply to each Global Note. While the Notes are represented by Global

Words in the Conditions that begin with a capital letter have the meanings given to them in the Conditions or
in the Incorporated Terms Memorandum described below. In the event of any inconsistency between the Conditions
and any Transaction Document, the Conditions prevail as regards the Noteholders, save that the Trust Deed
prevails in relation to the rights, powers and discretions of the Trustee.

## Condition 1 — Form, denomination and title

**(a) Form.** The Notes of each Class will be issued in fully registered form, without interest coupons. Each
Class will initially be represented by a global note in registered form (each, a "Global Note"), which will be
deposited on the Closing Date with, and registered in the name of a nominee of, a common depositary for one or
more international clearing systems (the "Clearing Systems"). Interests in a Global Note will be shown on, and
transfers of them will be effected only through, the records maintained by the Clearing Systems and their
participants. The Notes are not and will not be listed or admitted to trading on any stock exchange or market.

**(b) Denomination.** The Notes are issued in minimum denominations of £100,000 and integral multiples of £1,000
in excess thereof (each, an "Authorised Denomination"). No Note may be held or transferred in an amount lower
than £100,000. A Noteholder whose holding falls below £100,000 as a result of trading in the Clearing Systems
will not receive a Definitive Note for that holding and must first increase its holding to an Authorised
Denomination.

**(c) Definitive Notes.** A Global Note will be exchanged, in whole but not in part, for notes in definitive
registered form (the "Definitive Notes") only if: (i) both Clearing Systems are closed for business for a
continuous period of 14 days (other than by reason of a public holiday) or announce an intention permanently
to cease business, and no alternative clearing system satisfactory to the Trustee is available; or (ii) as a
result of a change in the law of the United Kingdom, or in its interpretation or administration, the Issuer or
any Paying Agent would be required to make a deduction or withholding for or on account of tax from a payment
on the Notes that would not be required if the Notes were in definitive form. The Issuer will issue Definitive
Notes, at its own cost, within 30 days of the occurrence of the relevant event. Definitive Notes will be
issued only in Authorised Denominations and will be serially numbered.

**(d) Title.** Title to the Notes passes only by registration in the register of Noteholders maintained by the
Registrar (the "Register"). The registered holder of a Note will (except as otherwise required by law) be
treated as its absolute owner for all purposes, whether or not any payment on it is overdue and regardless of
any notice of ownership, trust or any interest in it, any writing on it or its theft or loss, and no person
will be liable for so treating the holder. While the Notes are represented by a Global Note, each person shown
in the records of a Clearing System as the holder of a principal amount of Notes (an "Accountholder") must
look solely to that Clearing System for its share of each payment made to the registered holder, and the
Issuer, the Trustee and the Agents may treat the Accountholder as the holder of that principal amount for
every purpose other than payment.

**(e) Transfers of Definitive Notes.** A Definitive Note may be transferred in whole or in part (in an
Authorised Denomination) by depositing it at the specified office of the Registrar with the form of transfer
endorsed on it, duly completed and executed, together with any evidence the Registrar reasonably requires. A
new Definitive Note will be issued to the transferee, and a new Definitive Note for any balance to the
transferor, within five Business Days of receipt. Transfers will be effected without charge, except for any
tax or other governmental charge imposed in relation to the transfer. No transfer will be registered during
the 15 days ending on the due date for any payment of principal or interest on the Notes.

**(f) Selling restrictions.** The Notes may be offered, sold and transferred only to professional investors in
the United Kingdom and the European Economic Area, and only in compliance with the restrictions described in
section 20. Each purchaser of an interest in a Note is deemed to have made the representations set out in
section 20.

## Condition 2 — Status and ranking

**(a) Status.** The Notes are direct, secured, limited recourse and unconditional obligations of the Issuer,
secured by the Security described in Condition 3 and recoverable only as provided in Condition 11.

**(b) Ranking within a Class.** The Notes of each Class rank *pari passu* and rateably, without any preference
or priority among themselves, for all purposes.

**(c) Ranking between Classes.** Before the delivery of an Enforcement Notice, payments of interest and
principal on the Notes are made in the order set out in the Pre-Enforcement Priority of Payments (section 15).
In that order, interest on the Class A Notes is paid before interest on the Class B Notes, which is paid
before interest on the Class C Notes, which is paid before interest on the Class D Notes. Principal is paid
to all Classes *pro rata* to their Principal Amount Outstanding until the first Payment Date following the
occurrence of a Trigger Event and sequentially thereafter, as described in section 4 and section 16. After
the delivery of an Enforcement Notice, the Class A Notes rank in priority to the Class B Notes, the Class B
Notes in priority to the Class C Notes and the Class C Notes in priority to the Class D Notes, as to both
interest and principal, under the Post-Enforcement Priority of Payments.

**(d) Payments to other Secured Creditors.** The Issuer's obligations to the other Secured Creditors rank in
the order set out in the Pre-Enforcement Priority of Payments and the Post-Enforcement Priority of Payments.
The amounts due to the Servicer and the Trustee rank ahead of all payments on the Notes. Deferred consideration
payable to the Seller ranks behind all payments on the Notes.

**(e) Conflicts of interest between Classes.** The Trust Deed requires the Trustee, in exercising its powers,
trusts, authorities, duties and discretions, to have regard to the interests of the holders of each Class as a
class. Where, in the opinion of the Trustee, there is a conflict between the interests of the holders of two or
more Classes, the Trustee is required to have regard only to the interests of the holders of the Most Senior
Class then outstanding, and it will have no liability to the holders of any other Class for so doing. Where
there is a conflict between the interests of the Noteholders and those of any other Secured Creditor, the
Trustee is required to have regard only to the interests of the Noteholders. The Trustee is not required to
have regard to the interests of individual Noteholders arising from their particular circumstances, including
their place of residence or their tax position.

**(f) Retained Notes.** The Originator will hold, on the Closing Date and for the life of the transaction,
Notes of each Class equal to 5% of the nominal value of that Class, as its retained interest in the form of a
vertical slice described in section 18 (the "Retained Notes"). The Retained Notes rank *pari passu* with the
other Notes of the same Class and carry the same rights, save that, for as long as the Originator or any of its
affiliates holds them, they are disregarded for the purposes of determining the quorum and the result of any
vote on a resolution under Condition 12 that relates to the removal or replacement of the Servicer, the waiver
of a Servicer default, or any other matter in which the Originator or its affiliates has, in the opinion of the
Trustee, an interest that conflicts with the interests of the other Noteholders. Notes held by or for the
Issuer or Holdings are not treated as outstanding for any of those purposes.

## Condition 5 — Interest

**(a) Accrual of interest.** Each Note bears interest on its Principal Amount Outstanding from (and including)
the Closing Date. Interest is payable monthly in arrear on each Payment Date in respect of the Interest Period
ending on (but excluding) that Payment Date. Each Note (or, in the case of the redemption of part only of a
Note, that part) ceases to bear interest from its due date for redemption, unless payment of principal is
improperly withheld or refused, in which case it continues to bear interest in accordance with this Condition
(both before and after judgment) until whichever is the earlier of: (i) the day on which all sums due on that
Note up to that day are received by or on behalf of the relevant Noteholder; and (ii) the day falling five
Business Days after the Principal Paying Agent or the Trustee has notified the Noteholders, in accordance with
Condition 14, that it has received all sums due on that Note up to that fifth Business Day (except to the
extent that there is any subsequent default in payment).

**(b) Rate of Interest.** The rate of interest payable on each Class of Notes for each Interest Period (the
"Rate of Interest") is the sum of Compounded Daily SONIA for that Interest Period, determined by the Agent Bank
under Condition 5(c), and the margin for that Class set out in section 4 (the "Relevant Margin"). The Relevant
Margin of each Class is fixed on the Closing Date for the life of the Notes and does not step up. If the Rate of
Interest so determined for any Class and any Interest Period is less than zero, the Rate of Interest for that
Class and that Interest Period will be zero.

**(c) Compounded Daily SONIA.** "Compounded Daily SONIA" means, for an Interest Period, the rate of return of a
daily compound interest investment in sterling (with the daily Sterling Overnight Index Average as the
reference rate for the calculation of interest), calculated by the Agent Bank on the Interest Determination
Date for that Interest Period as follows, the resulting percentage being rounded, if necessary, to the fourth
decimal place, with 0.00005 being rounded upwards:

(i) for each London Banking Day in the Interest Period, the Agent Bank takes the SONIA rate for the London
    Banking Day falling five London Banking Days before it (the "Lookback Rate"), multiplies it by the number
    of calendar days from (and including) that London Banking Day to (but excluding) the next London Banking
    Day, divides the result by 365, and adds one;
(ii) the Agent Bank then multiplies together all the factors obtained under paragraph (i) for the London
    Banking Days in the Interest Period, and subtracts one from the product; and
(iii) the Agent Bank multiplies the result by 365 and divides it by the number of calendar days in the
    Interest Period.

In this Condition: "**SONIA**" means, for any London Banking Day, a reference rate equal to the daily Sterling
Overnight Index Average rate for that London Banking Day as provided by the Bank of England, as administrator
of that rate (or any successor administrator), to authorised distributors and as then published on the
relevant screen page designated by those distributors (the "Relevant Screen Page") or, if the Relevant Screen
Page is unavailable, as otherwise published by those authorised distributors, in each case on the London
Banking Day immediately following that London Banking Day; "**London Banking Day**" means any day on which
commercial banks are open for general business (including dealing in foreign exchange and foreign currency
deposits) in London; and "**Interest Determination Date**" means, for each Interest Period, the fifth London
Banking Day before the Payment Date on which that Interest Period ends.

The lookback of five London Banking Days is applied to the SONIA rate only: the days on which the rate is
weighted and the number of days in the Interest Period are those of the Interest Period itself. The
"Compounded Daily SONIA" so determined for each Interest Period is reported in the monthly investor report as
the SONIA rate for the related Collection Period.

**(d) SONIA fallback.** If, in respect of any London Banking Day, the Agent Bank determines that SONIA is not
made available on the Relevant Screen Page and has not otherwise been published by the authorised
distributors, SONIA for that London Banking Day will be: (i) the Bank of England's Bank Rate (the "Bank Rate")
prevailing at close of business on that London Banking Day; plus (ii) the mean of the spread of SONIA to the
Bank Rate over the previous five days on which SONIA has been published, excluding the highest spread (or, if
there is more than one highest spread, one only of those highest spreads) and the lowest spread (or, if there
is more than one lowest spread, one only of those lowest spreads). Notwithstanding the preceding sentence, if
the Bank of England publishes guidance as to how SONIA is to be determined, or as to any rate that is to
replace SONIA, the Agent Bank will, to the extent that it is reasonably practicable, follow that guidance in
order to determine SONIA for so long as SONIA is not available or has not been published by the authorised
distributors.

If the Rate of Interest cannot be determined in accordance with Conditions 5(c) and 5(d) by the Agent Bank,
the Rate of Interest for the relevant Interest Period will be the Rate of Interest last determined for the
relevant Class for the immediately preceding Interest Period (or, if there has been no preceding Interest
Period, the rate which would have applied to the first Interest Period had the Notes been in issue for a full
month ending on the Closing Date). The application of this paragraph is without prejudice to the operation of
Condition 12(g) on the occurrence of a Benchmark Rate Disruption Event.

**(e) Interest Amount.** The Agent Bank will, as soon as practicable after the Interest Determination Date for
each Interest Period, calculate the amount of interest payable on each Class of Notes for that Interest Period
(the "Interest Amount"). The Interest Amount of a Class is the Principal Amount Outstanding of that Class on the
first day of the Interest Period (after taking into account any payment of principal made on that day)
multiplied by one-twelfth of the Rate of Interest of that Class, the result being rounded to the nearest penny
(half a penny being rounded upwards). The same fraction of one-twelfth applies to every Interest Period,
including the first Interest Period and any Interest Period that is lengthened or shortened because a Payment
Date is not a Business Day. The Interest Amount payable on each Note of a Class is the Interest Amount of that
Class multiplied by the fraction that the Principal Amount Outstanding of that Note represents of the Principal
Amount Outstanding of the Class, rounded down to the nearest penny.

## Condition 6 — Redemption

**(a) Final redemption.** Unless previously redeemed in full and cancelled as provided in this Condition, the
Issuer will redeem the Notes of each Class at their Principal Amount Outstanding, together with accrued
interest, on the Payment Date falling on [[final_maturity_date]] (the "Final Maturity Date"). The Issuer may not
redeem or purchase Notes other than in accordance with this Condition. The Final Maturity Date falls after the
latest scheduled maturity of any Loan Agreement in the Portfolio, to allow time for the collection of balloon
payments, the recovery of amounts due on defaulted Loan Agreements and the sale of the related Financed
Vehicles.

**(b) Mandatory redemption in part.** On each Payment Date before the delivery of an Enforcement Notice, the
Issuer will redeem the Notes in part by applying, in accordance with the Pre-Enforcement Priority of Payments,
the amount of Available Funds remaining after the payment of the items ranking in priority to principal on the
Notes (the "Principal Payment Amount"). The Principal Payment Amount for each Payment Date is limited to the
amount needed to reduce the aggregate Principal Amount Outstanding of the Notes to the Pool Balance at the end
of the immediately preceding Collection Period (the "Target Principal Amount"). The Principal Payment Amount is
applied:

(i) until the first Payment Date following the occurrence of a Trigger Event, *pro rata* to the Principal
    Amount Outstanding of the Class A Notes, the Class B Notes, the Class C Notes and the Class D Notes; and
(ii) from (and including) the first Payment Date following the occurrence of a Trigger Event, sequentially:
    first to the Class A Notes until they are redeemed in full, then to the Class B Notes until they are
    redeemed in full, then to the Class C Notes until they are redeemed in full and finally to the Class D
    Notes, as described in section 4 and section 16.

The change from *pro rata* to sequential redemption under paragraph (ii) is permanent and does not reverse if
the Trigger Event ceases to apply. The amount applied to each Class is apportioned among the Notes of that
Class *pro rata* to their Principal Amount Outstanding, rounded down to the nearest penny.

**(c) Principal deficiency.** If the Principal Payment Amount for any Payment Date is less than the amount needed
to reduce the aggregate Principal Amount Outstanding of the Notes to the Target Principal Amount, the excess of
the aggregate Principal Amount Outstanding of the Notes (after the payments made on that Payment Date) over the
Pool Balance at the end of the preceding Collection Period is a "Principal Deficiency". A Principal Deficiency
is not an Event of Default. It is reduced on later Payment Dates by the application of Available Funds in the
manner described in section 15 and, to the extent not so reduced, it is borne by the Notes in reverse order of
seniority on the Final Maturity Date or on enforcement. The Cash Manager will record the amount of any
Principal Deficiency on each Calculation Date and the Servicer will report it in the monthly investor report.

**(d) Optional redemption: clean-up call.** On any Payment Date on which the Pool Balance at the end of the
immediately preceding Collection Period is less than 10% of the Pool Balance at the Cut-off Date, Kestrel Motor
Finance Ltd, as Seller, may under the Sale Agreement repurchase all (but not some) of the Loan
Agreements then held by the Issuer, together with all the related Receivables and the Issuer's interest in the
related Financed Vehicles (the "Clean-Up Call"). The repurchase price paid by the Seller funds the redemption of

Payment Date at their Principal Amount Outstanding together with accrued and unpaid interest (including any

(i) the Issuer has given not more than 60 nor fewer than 30 days' notice to the Trustee and to the Noteholders
    in accordance with Condition 14;
(ii) the repurchase price payable by the Seller is not less than the amount needed to redeem all the Notes as
    described above and to pay all amounts ranking in priority to or *pari passu* with the Notes in the
    Post-Enforcement Priority of Payments; and
(iii) before giving the notice, the Issuer has certified to the Trustee that it will have the necessary funds

    enquiry.

The repurchase price for a defaulted Receivable is its fair market value, determined by the Servicer in
accordance with its credit and collection policies; for every other Receivable it is the outstanding principal
balance plus accrued interest. The Clean-Up Call is the only optional redemption of the Notes apart from

Amount Outstanding of the relevant Class (or, at an adjourned meeting, at least 25%).

**(d) Basic Terms Modifications.** A "**Basic Terms Modification**" is a modification that would: (i) change any
date fixed for payment of principal or interest on any Class, including the Final Maturity Date; (ii) reduce
the amount of principal or interest payable on any Class, or change the method of calculating it, other than a
Benchmark Rate Modification under Condition 12(g); (iii) change the currency of payment of any Class; (iv) alter
the Pre-Enforcement Priority of Payments or the Post-Enforcement Priority of Payments in a way that affects the
amount or timing of payments on any Class; (v) release the Security other than as permitted by the
Transaction Documents; (vi) change the quorum required at any meeting or the majority required to pass an
Extraordinary Resolution; or (vii) amend this definition. A Basic Terms Modification takes effect only if
sanctioned by an Extraordinary Resolution of the holders of each Class then outstanding.

**(e) Effect on other Classes.** An Extraordinary Resolution passed by the holders of the Most Senior Class is
binding on the holders of all the other Classes, irrespective of its effect on them, except an Extraordinary
Resolution sanctioning a Basic Terms Modification. No Extraordinary Resolution of the holders of any other Class
is effective unless the Trustee is of the opinion that it will not be materially prejudicial to the interests
of the holders of each Class ranking above it, or it is sanctioned by an Extraordinary Resolution of the holders
of each such Class. A resolution passed at a meeting of a Class is binding on all the holders of that Class,
whether or not they were present or voted.

**(f) Modification and waiver without the consent of Noteholders.** The Trustee may agree, without the consent or
sanction of the Noteholders or the other Secured Creditors, to: (i) any modification of the Conditions or of
any Transaction Document (other than a Basic Terms Modification) that, in the opinion of the Trustee, is not
materially prejudicial to the interests of the holders of the Most Senior Class; (ii) any modification that, in

benchmark rate on the Notes from SONIA to an alternative benchmark rate (an "Alternative Benchmark Rate") and to

"**Benchmark Rate Modification**"), provided that:

(ii) the Issuer has given at least 30 days' notice of the proposed Benchmark Rate Modification to the

unless it is sanctioned by an Extraordinary Resolution of the holders of the Most Senior Class (for which
purpose the quorum and majority for an Extraordinary Resolution that is not a Basic Terms Modification apply).
A "**Benchmark Rate Disruption Event**" means any of: (i) a material disruption to SONIA, a material change in
its methodology or SONIA ceasing to exist or to be published; (ii) a public statement by the administrator of
SONIA that it has ceased or will cease to publish SONIA permanently or indefinitely (where no successor
administrator has been appointed); (iii) a public statement by the supervisor of the administrator of SONIA that
SONIA has been or will be permanently or indefinitely discontinued, prohibited from being used or is no longer
representative of its underlying market; (iv) it becoming unlawful for the Agent Bank, the Issuer or any Paying

## Condition 13 — Replacement of notes

If a Definitive Note is mutilated, defaced, lost, stolen or destroyed, it may be replaced at the specified
office of the Registrar, subject to all applicable laws, on payment by the claimant of the expenses incurred in
connection with the replacement and on such terms as to evidence, security and indemnity as the Issuer and the
Registrar may reasonably require. A mutilated or defaced Definitive Note must be surrendered before a
replacement is issued. If a Definitive Note that has been replaced is subsequently presented for payment, the
claimant must pay to the Issuer on demand the amount the Issuer pays on that Note. A Global Note that is
mutilated, defaced, lost, stolen or destroyed will be replaced by the Issuer on the same terms, without any
expense to the Accountholders.

## Condition 15 — Substitution of the issuer

**(a) Substitution.** The Trustee may agree with the Issuer, without the consent of the Noteholders or the other
Secured Creditors, to the substitution of another company (the "Substituted Obligor") in place of the Issuer
as principal debtor under the Notes, the Trust Deed and the other Transaction Documents, provided that:

(i) a deed is executed, or some other form of undertaking is given, by the Substituted Obligor, in a form and
    manner satisfactory to the Trustee, agreeing to be bound by the Notes, the Trust Deed and the other
    Transaction Documents as if it had been named in them as principal debtor;
(ii) the Substituted Obligor acquires the Issuer's interest in the Receivables and its other assets, and grants
    security over them in favour of the Trustee equivalent to the Security;
(iii) the Substituted Obligor is a special purpose company, with restrictions on its business equivalent to
    those in Condition 4, and its directors certify that it will be solvent immediately after the
    substitution;
(iv) the Trustee is satisfied that the substitution will not be materially prejudicial to the interests of the
    holders of the Most Senior Class, and Rating Agency Confirmation has been obtained for the substitution;
(v) where the Substituted Obligor is incorporated, domiciled or resident for tax purposes in a territory other
    than the United Kingdom, undertakings or covenants are given in terms corresponding to Condition 8 and
    Condition 6(e), with references to that territory in addition to the United Kingdom, and legal opinions
    satisfactory to the Trustee are delivered as to the law of that territory; and
(vi) the substitution complies with the UK Securitisation Framework and does not cause the Originator to cease to
    comply with its retention obligation described in Condition 2(f).

**(b) Effect.** On the substitution taking effect, the Substituted Obligor will be treated as the Issuer for every
purpose, and the Issuer will be released from its obligations as principal debtor. The Issuer will give notice
of the substitution to the Noteholders in accordance with Condition 14 within 14 days of its taking effect. In
connection with any substitution, the Trustee will not have regard to the consequences for individual
Noteholders resulting from their being domiciled or resident in, or otherwise connected with, any particular
territory, and no Noteholder is entitled to claim from the Issuer, the Substituted Obligor or the Trustee any
indemnification or payment in respect of any tax consequence of the substitution for that Noteholder.
