# 6. Use of proceeds

The gross proceeds of the issue of the Notes on the Closing Date will be equal to the aggregate initial
principal amount of the Notes set out in section 4, which is equal to the pool balance at the Cut-off Date
([[pool_balance]]). The Issuer will apply the whole of those proceeds on the Closing Date to pay to the Seller
the initial purchase price for the [[n_loans]] loans in the portfolio and their related rights, under the Loan
Sale Agreement (the "Initial Purchase Price"). The Seller will apply part of the Initial Purchase Price to repay
the amounts outstanding under the private warehouse facility from which certain of the Loans are released on the
Closing Date, as described in section 8, and will receive the balance for its general corporate purposes.

In addition to the Initial Purchase Price, the Issuer will pay to the Seller deferred consideration for the
Loans (the "Deferred Consideration"). Deferred Consideration is payable on each Payment Date only out of the
Available Funds that remain after every higher-ranking item in the Priority of Payments has been paid in full,
as set out in section 15. The Seller has no claim against the Issuer for Deferred Consideration on any Payment
Date on which those funds are insufficient.

The reserve fund described in section 16 will be funded by the Originator on the Closing Date and does not
reduce the proceeds applied to the Initial Purchase Price. The fees and expenses of the Arranger and the Lead
Manager and the other costs incurred in connection with the issue of the Notes will be paid by the Originator
and will not be paid out of the proceeds of the Notes. None of the proceeds will be retained by the Issuer as
profit, and the Issuer will not use any part of them for any other purpose.
