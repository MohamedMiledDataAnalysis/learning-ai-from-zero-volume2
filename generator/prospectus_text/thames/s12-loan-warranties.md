# 12. Loan warranties and repurchase

## The Sale Agreement

On the Closing Date, Kestrel Motor Finance Ltd ("Kestrel", in this capacity the "Seller") will sell the Loans in
the Portfolio to Thames Auto Receivables 2026-1 plc (the "Issuer") under a receivables sale agreement dated the
Closing Date between the Seller, the Issuer and the Trustee (the "Sale Agreement"). The sale takes effect, in
relation to each Loan, from the Cut-off Date, so that all collections received by the Seller on or after the
Cut-off Date in respect of the Loans belong to the Issuer, whether received before or after the Closing Date.
The Sale Agreement is governed by English law, save that the declaration of trust by which the Seller holds the
Loans whose Borrowers reside in Scotland (the "Scottish Loans") on trust for the Issuer (the "Scottish
Declaration of Trust") is governed by Scots law, as is required for it to take effect.

The property sold comprises, for each Loan, the Seller's rights under the related HP Agreement or PCP Agreement
to receive all instalments, Balloon Payments, settlement amounts, arrears, fees and other sums payable by the
Borrower, together with the Seller's rights against the Introducing Dealer and any insurer in relation to the
Loan, the proceeds of any claim, and the Seller's rights in respect of the financed vehicle, including the
proceeds of its sale after repossession, surrender, voluntary termination or return at the end of a PCP
Agreement (together, the "Ancillary Rights"). Title to each vehicle remains registered in the records of the
Seller. Because the transfer of legal title to vehicles would require notification of each Borrower, the Seller
will declare a trust over its title to the vehicles and over the proceeds of their sale in favour of the Issuer
under the Sale Agreement (the "Vehicle Trust"), and will hold that title, and apply any proceeds, as trustee for
the Issuer.

The consideration for the sale consists of an initial purchase price paid in cash on the Closing Date from the
proceeds of the issue of the Notes (the "Initial Purchase Price"), equal to the aggregate outstanding principal
of the Loans at the Cut-off Date, and of deferred consideration payable to the Seller on each Payment Date to the
extent of the Available Funds remaining after the payment of the items ranking in priority to it in the Priority
of Payments described in section 15 (the "Deferred Consideration"). The Seller has no claim against the Issuer
for any Deferred Consideration which is not paid because the Available Funds are insufficient.

The Sale Agreement contains the representations and warranties described below, the Seller's obligation to
repurchase Loans which do not comply with them, the obligations of the Seller relating to commission redress
and set-off described in section 8, and the provisions on the perfection of the Issuer's title.

## Representations of the Seller as to itself

The Seller represents and warrants to the Issuer and the Trustee on the date of the Sale Agreement and on the
Closing Date that:

(a) it is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of England and Wales and has the power to
enter into and perform the Transaction Documents to which it is a party;

(b) its obligations under those Transaction Documents are legal, valid, binding and enforceable, subject to
the laws on insolvency and creditors' rights and to the general principles of law;

(c) it holds all authorisations required under FSMA and the CCA to originate, own and service the Loans, and
no such authorisation has been suspended, varied or cancelled;

(d) it is not unable to pay its debts within the meaning of section 123 of the Insolvency Act 1986 and will not
become so as a result of the sale of the Loans, which is made for full value and in good faith.

## Loan warranties

In addition, the Seller will represent and warrant to the Issuer and the Trustee in relation to each Loan, as at
the Cut-off Date or, where stated, the Closing Date (together, the "Loan Warranties"), that the Loan satisfied
each of the Eligibility Criteria set out in section 11 and that:

(a) the Loan was originated by the Seller in the ordinary course of its business, in accordance with the Credit
and Collection Policies described in section 9 as they applied at the date of origination, and through an
Introducing Dealer which was, at that date, authorised by the FCA, or an appointed representative of a firm so
authorised, for the introduction of regulated credit agreements;

(b) the Finance Agreement was entered into in the Seller's standard form of HP Agreement or PCP Agreement in
use at that date, contains all prescribed terms and complies in all material respects with the CCA and the
regulations made under it, so that it is enforceable against the Borrower without an order of the court under
section 127 of the CCA;

(c) the pre-contract information and the adequate explanations required by the CCA and CONC were given to the
Borrower, a creditworthiness assessment was carried out in accordance with CONC 5.2A, and the Borrower's right of
withdrawal under section 66A of the CCA has expired without being exercised;

(d) the Seller is the sole legal and beneficial owner of the Loan and of the Ancillary Rights and has full title
to the vehicle, free from any security interest, trust, option or other third party right, other than the
rights of the Borrower under the Finance Agreement and any security which is released on or before the Closing
Date;

(e) the Loan and the Ancillary Rights are capable of being sold to the Issuer, and of being held on trust for
it, without the consent of the Borrower or any other person and without breach of any restriction on transfer;

(f) the Loan bears interest at a fixed rate, is denominated and payable in sterling, and is repayable by monthly
instalments and, for a PCP Agreement, a Balloon Payment equal to the GMFV set at origination;

(g) the vehicle was a car which, at origination, was not recorded as stolen, written off or subject to
outstanding finance, was delivered to the Borrower and accepted by the Borrower, and is registered with the
industry vehicle finance register as subject to the Finance Agreement;

(h) no Borrower has notified the Seller of any rejection of the vehicle under the Consumer Rights Act 2015, of
any claim under section 75 or section 56 of the CCA or of any claim for rescission, and, so far as the Seller is
aware, no Borrower has a right of set-off or counterclaim against the Seller, other than a claim in respect of
commission which is subject to the redress scheme and indemnity described in section 8;

(i) the Seller has not received notice of the death or bankruptcy of any Borrower, or of any voluntary
termination under section 99 of the CCA, and the Loan is not subject to a forbearance arrangement, a payment
holiday or a variation of its contractual terms which remains in effect at the Cut-off Date;

(j) the Loan is not a Defaulted Loan, has not been the subject of a default notice under section 87 of the CCA
and has not been written off in whole or in part;

(k) the Borrower is not an employee, director or connected person of the Seller and is not, so far as the
Seller is aware, the subject of any insolvency proceedings;

(l) the Loan Files for the Loan are complete in all material respects and are held by the Seller in accordance
with section 9, and the information relating to the Loan in the data tape from which section 10 was prepared
is true and accurate in all material respects;

(m) the Loan has been serviced by the Seller since its origination in accordance with the Credit and Collection
Policies, the CCA, CONC and the Consumer Duty, and the Seller has complied in all material respects with its
obligations to send the statements, notices and information required by the CCA; and

(n) the Loan has not been selected for sale to the Issuer by a process intended to select assets of lower credit
quality than comparable Finance Agreements of the Seller which satisfy the Eligibility Criteria.

The Loan Warranties are given by reference to the facts at the Cut-off Date or the Closing Date and are not
repeated after the Closing Date. The Seller gives no warranty as to the future performance of any Loan, the
creditworthiness of any Borrower or the value of any vehicle, and the Issuer bears the credit risk and the
residual value risk of the Loans, subject only to the remedies described below. The Issuer and the Trustee rely on the Loan Warranties and have made no investigation of the Loans other
than the agreed-upon procedures described in section 8.

## Repurchase of non-compliant Loans

If the Seller, the Servicer, the Issuer, the Cash Manager or the Trustee becomes aware that a Loan Warranty was
untrue in relation to a Loan when it was given, it must notify the other parties promptly. Where the breach has a
material adverse effect on the value of the Loan or on the interests of the Issuer in it, and, if capable of
remedy, is not remedied within 30 days of the Seller becoming aware of it or receiving notice of it, the Seller
must repurchase the Loan (a "Non-Compliant Loan") together with its Ancillary Rights on or before the Payment Date
following the end of that period. A breach of the warranty that a Loan satisfied the Eligibility Criteria is
treated as having a material adverse effect, whatever the performance of the Loan.

The price payable by the Seller for a Non-Compliant Loan (the "Repurchase Price") is the outstanding principal
of the Loan on the date of repurchase, plus accrued and unpaid interest and any other amounts due from the
Borrower, plus the costs reasonably incurred by the Issuer in connection with the repurchase. Where the
Non-Compliant Loan has become a Defaulted Loan, the Repurchase Price is calculated on the outstanding principal
at the date on which it became a Defaulted Loan, less any Recoveries already received by the Issuer. The
Repurchase Price is paid into the Issuer Transaction Account described in section 14 and forms part of the
Available Funds for the relevant period, as principal received in respect of the outstanding principal and as
interest collections in respect of the remainder.

Where the Seller is unable to repurchase a Non-Compliant Loan, for example because the Finance Agreement has
been settled or the vehicle has been sold, the Seller must instead pay the Issuer an indemnity amount equal to
the Repurchase Price that would have been payable. The Seller's obligations to repurchase a Non-Compliant Loan
or pay that indemnity amount, and its indemnity in respect of commission redress and set-off described in
section 8, are the sole remedies of the Issuer and the Trustee for a breach of the Loan Warranties. These
obligations are unsecured obligations of the Seller and are not guaranteed by the Parent or any other person;
their value to the Issuer depends on the solvency of the Seller at the time of the claim, as explained in
section 2.

The Seller must also repurchase a Loan, at the Repurchase Price, if the Servicer agrees a variation of the
Finance Agreement which is not permitted by the Servicing Agreement (see section 13), and may repurchase all, but
not some only, of the Loans on any Payment Date on which the Issuer may redeem the Notes under the clean-up call
described in section 5, at a price which, together with the other Available Funds, is sufficient to redeem the
Notes in full and pay all amounts ranking in priority to them.

## No substitution and no further sales

The Portfolio is static. The Seller has no right or obligation to substitute another Finance Agreement for a
Non-Compliant Loan or for any other Loan, and the Issuer will not purchase any further loans after the Closing
Date. Apart from a repurchase described above, the Seller has no right to repurchase or call any Loan, and the
Issuer has no right to require the Seller to repurchase a Loan because it has become a Defaulted Loan or has
otherwise performed badly.

## Notification and perfection of the Issuer's title

The Seller will not, on the Closing Date, give notice of the sale to the Borrowers, the Introducing Dealers or
any insurer. Until notice is given, the sale of the Loans which are governed by English law or the law of
Northern Ireland takes effect as an equitable assignment, and the Issuer's interest in the Scottish Loans takes
effect under the Scottish Declaration of Trust. As a result, until notice is given or an assignation is
intimated:

(a) a Borrower may continue to pay the Seller and obtains a good discharge by doing so;

(b) a Borrower may set off against the Seller claims which arise before the Borrower receives notice, and the
Issuer's rights may be subject to equities which arise between the Borrower and the Seller;

(c) the Seller remains the legal owner of the Loans and the vehicles and must be joined in any proceedings
taken by the Issuer to enforce a Loan; and

(d) a later legal assignee of a Loan without notice of the Issuer's interest could obtain priority over it.

The Issuer is protected against these risks by the Seller's covenants in the Sale Agreement, the Vehicle Trust,
the declaration of trust over the Collection Account described in section 14 and the Seller's obligation to pay
any amount set off by a Borrower as a deemed collection. The Seller will grant to the Issuer and the Trustee
an irrevocable power of attorney, by way of security for its obligations, to perfect the transfer of the Loans
and the vehicles in its name after a Perfection Event.

Each of the following events is a "Perfection Event":

(a) a Servicer Termination Event occurs, as described in section 13, and the appointment of Kestrel as Servicer
is terminated;

(b) an Insolvency Event occurs in relation to the Seller;

(c) the Seller fails to pay any amount due under the Sale Agreement, including a Repurchase Price, within five
Business Days of its due date;

(d) the Trustee delivers an enforcement notice to the Issuer under the Conditions;

(e) the Seller is required to perfect the Issuer's title by law, by an order of a court or by a requirement of a
regulatory authority; or

(f) the Trustee, acting reasonably, considers that the security created by the Issuer, or the Issuer's title to
a material part of the Loans, is in jeopardy.

Within 20 Business Days after a Perfection Event, the Seller (or, if it fails to do so, the Issuer or the
Trustee under the power of attorney) must give notice of the assignment to each Borrower and instruct each
Borrower to pay directly into an account of the Issuer, execute legal assignments and assignations of the
Loans, intimate the assignation of the Scottish Loans to the Borrowers and transfer to the Issuer, or as it
directs, legal title to the vehicles and the Loan Files. The costs of perfection are borne by the Seller. After
notice, payments by a Borrower to the Seller do not discharge the Borrower, and new rights of set-off against
the Seller cease to arise. Notice to Borrowers will be given in a form which complies with the CCA, CONC and
the Consumer Duty, and the Borrowers' rights under their Finance Agreements, including the right to voluntary
termination and the protections described in section 13, will not be affected by the transfer.
