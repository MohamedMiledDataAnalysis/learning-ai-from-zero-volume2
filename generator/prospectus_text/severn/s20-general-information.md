## Section 20 — General Information {-}

### Use of Proceeds

The gross proceeds of the issue of the Notes on the Issue Date will be equal to the aggregate principal amount of
the Notes set out in Section 3, which is equal to the aggregate current balance of the Provisional Portfolio on
the Portfolio Reference Date ([[pool_balance]]). The Issuer will apply the whole of those proceeds on the Issue
Date in paying to the Seller the initial consideration for the Mortgage Loans and their Related Security under
the Mortgage Sale Agreement (the "Initial Consideration"). The Seller will be entitled, in addition, to deferred
consideration, payable only to the extent that Available Revenue Receipts remain after all items ranking above it
in the Revenue Priority of Payments have been paid in full, as set out in Section 6.

The proceeds of the Subordinated Loan will be applied on the Issue Date in funding the General Reserve Fund and
the Liquidity Reserve Fund up to their respective required amounts, as described in Section 5. The commissions,
fees and expenses of the Arranger and the other expenses incurred in connection with the issue of the Notes will
be paid by the Society and will not be paid out of the proceeds of the Notes.

### Authorisation

The issue of the Notes and the entry by the Issuer into the Transaction Documents to which it is a party were
authorised by resolutions of the board of directors of the Issuer passed on 5 May 2026. The entry by Holdings
into its covenants in favour of the Security Trustee was authorised by its board of directors on the same date.
The entry by the Society into the Transaction Documents to which it is a party was authorised by its board of
directors in accordance with its rules and the Building Societies Act 1986. The Issuer has obtained all
necessary consents, approvals and authorisations in the United Kingdom in connection with the issue and
performance of the Notes.

### Litigation

The Issuer is not, and has not been since its incorporation, involved in any governmental, legal or arbitration
proceedings (including any such proceedings which are pending or threatened of which the Issuer is aware) which
may have, or have had, a significant effect on its financial position or profitability. Holdings makes the same
statement in respect of itself. The Society is, in the ordinary course of its business, the subject of
complaints by borrowers, including complaints referred to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and of possession and
other proceedings brought by it as lender; the Society does not consider that any such proceedings, alone or
together, are likely to have a material adverse effect on its ability to perform its obligations under the
Transaction Documents.

### No Significant Change

Since the date of its incorporation, there has been (a) no material adverse change in the financial position or
prospects of the Issuer, and (b) no significant change in the financial or trading position of the Issuer. The
Issuer has not, since its incorporation, entered into any contract other than in the ordinary course of its
business or as described in Section 9.

### Accounts

No statutory accounts of the Issuer or of Holdings have been prepared as at the date of this Offering Circular.
The first financial period of each will end on 31 December 2026. The Issuer will publish its audited annual
accounts within the period required by the Companies Act 2006 and will deliver copies of them to the Note
Trustee. The Auditors are Fennick & Rowe LLP, as described in Section 9. The Issuer does not intend to publish
interim accounts.

### Investor Reporting

The Cash Manager will prepare the Investor Report for each quarterly collection period and publish it after each
Interest Payment Date, as described in Section 14. Each Investor Report will set out, among other things, the
performance of the Mortgage Loans, the Arrears Ratio and the Cumulative Loss Ratio, the application of the
Available Revenue Receipts and the Available Principal Receipts, the balances of the Principal Deficiency Ledger
and of the reserve funds, the principal amount outstanding of each Class of Notes and the status of each Trigger
Event described in Section 8.

### Post-Issuance Reporting under the UK Securitisation Framework

The Issuer has been designated, in accordance with the Securitisation Regulations 2024 and SECN 6, as the entity
responsible for fulfilling the transparency requirements of the UK securitisation framework (the "Reporting
Entity"), and the Society has agreed to provide it with the information it requires for that purpose. The
Reporting Entity will make the following available to the Noteholders, the competent authorities and, on request,
potential investors, through a securitisation repository or, where none is available, a website that satisfies
the requirements of SECN 6:

(a) before pricing, and in final form no later than 15 days after the Issue Date, the Transaction Documents and
this Offering Circular;

(b) quarterly, loan-level information on the Mortgage Loans, in the form required by SECN 6, published at the same
time as the Investor Report;

(c) quarterly, an investor report in the form required by SECN 6, including the confirmation by the Society of its
continued retention of the randomly selected exposures described in Section 17; and

(d) without delay, any significant event, including a material breach of the Transaction Documents, a change in
the structural features or risk characteristics of the Transaction, the occurrence of a Sequential Payment
Trigger, the termination of the appointment of the Mortgage Administrator, the Cash Manager or the Swap Provider,
and any material amendment to the Transaction Documents.

The information will be kept available for at least five years after the Final Maturity Date. The risk retention
and other regulatory disclosures that apply to the Transaction are described in Section 17.
