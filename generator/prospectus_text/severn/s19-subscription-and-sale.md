## Section 19 — Subscription and Sale {-}

### The Subscription Agreement

Under a subscription agreement dated on or about the date of this Offering Circular between the Issuer, the
Society and the Arranger (the "Subscription Agreement"), the Arranger has agreed with the Issuer, subject to the
satisfaction of certain conditions, to subscribe or procure subscribers for the Notes on the Issue Date at an
issue price of 100 per cent of their principal amount. The Society has agreed in the Subscription Agreement to
purchase on the Issue Date any Notes which it has notified to the Arranger that it wishes to acquire, and the
obligation of the Arranger applies to the remaining Notes of each Class. The retention of a material net
economic interest by the Society is effected as described in Section 17,
and not by holding Notes.

The obligations of the Arranger under the Subscription Agreement are subject to conditions precedent, including:

(a) the execution and delivery of the Transaction Documents by each of the parties to them on or before the
Issue Date;

(b) the delivery to the Arranger of legal opinions from Hartwell Quinn LLP and Grantley Moss LLP, as to English
law, Scots law and United Kingdom taxation, in a form satisfactory to the Arranger;

(c) the assignment of the ratings set out in Section 3 to the Rated Notes by the Rating Agencies, and the absence,
before the Issue Date, of any downgrade, or of any public announcement of a possible downgrade, of those
ratings;

(d) the accuracy, on the date of the Subscription Agreement and on the Issue Date, of the representations and
warranties given by the Issuer and the Society, and the performance by each of them of the obligations to be
performed by it on or before the Issue Date; and

(e) the absence, before the Issue Date, of any material adverse change in the financial condition, business or
prospects of the Issuer or the Society, or of any change in national or international financial, political or
economic conditions which, in the opinion of the Arranger, is likely to prejudice materially the success of the
offering or the distribution of the Notes.

The Issuer and the Society have given representations and warranties to the Arranger in respect of, among other
things, themselves, the Mortgage Loans, the Transaction Documents and the information contained in this Offering
Circular. The Society has agreed to indemnify the Arranger against certain liabilities and expenses incurred in
connection with the issue of the Notes and to pay the commissions, fees and expenses of the Arranger and the
other expenses of the issue, as described in Section 20. The Arranger may terminate the Subscription Agreement
in certain circumstances at any time before payment of the subscription moneys to the Issuer.

The Notes will be delivered on the Issue Date ([[issue_date]]) against payment, through the Clearing Systems
described in Section 20.

### General

The Arranger has represented, warranted and undertaken that it has complied and will comply with all applicable
laws and regulations in each jurisdiction in which it purchases, offers, sells or delivers Notes or possesses,
distributes or publishes this Offering Circular or any other offering material relating to the Notes, and that
it will obtain any consent, approval or permission required by it for the purchase, offer, sale or delivery of
Notes under the laws and regulations in force in any jurisdiction to which it is subject or in which it makes
such purchases, offers, sales or deliveries.

No action has been taken by the Issuer, the Society or the Arranger which would, or is intended to, permit a
public offer of the Notes, or the possession or distribution of this Offering Circular or any other offering
material, in any country or jurisdiction where action for that purpose is required. Neither the Issuer nor the
Arranger represents that the Notes may at any time lawfully be sold in compliance with any registration or other
requirement in any jurisdiction, or pursuant to any exemption available there, or assumes any responsibility for
facilitating any such sale.

### United Kingdom

The Arranger has represented, warranted and undertaken that:

(a) it has only communicated or caused to be communicated, and will only communicate or cause to be
communicated, any invitation or inducement to engage in investment activity (within the meaning of section 21
of FSMA) received by it in connection with the issue or sale of any Notes in circumstances in which section 21(1)
of FSMA does not apply to the Issuer;

(b) it has complied and will comply with all applicable provisions of FSMA with respect to anything done by it in
relation to the Notes in, from or otherwise involving the United Kingdom;

(c) it has not made and will not make an offer of Notes to the public in the United Kingdom, except an offer
falling within an exception in Schedule 1 to the Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regulations 2024,
including an offer made only to qualified investors, provided that no such offer requires the Issuer or the
Arranger to publish a prospectus or other offering document;

(d) it has not offered, sold or otherwise made available, and will not offer, sell or otherwise make available,
any Notes to any retail investor in the United Kingdom, as defined in the Important Notice; and

(e) it will not sell any Notes to a retail client in the United Kingdom otherwise than in accordance with the
restrictions on the sale of securitisation positions to retail clients in the Securitisation Regulations 2024
and SECN.

This Offering Circular is directed only at persons in the United Kingdom who: (a) have professional experience in
matters relating to investments falling within Article 19(5) of the Financial Services and Markets Act 2000
(Financial Promotion) Order 2005 (the "Financial Promotion Order"); (b) fall within Article 49(2)(a) to (d) of
the Financial Promotion Order; or (c) are persons to whom it may otherwise lawfully be communicated (together,
"Relevant Persons"). This Offering Circular must not be acted on or relied on by persons who are not Relevant
Persons, and any investment or investment activity to which it relates is available only to, and will be engaged
in only with, Relevant Persons.

### European Economic Area

In relation to each member state of the EEA, the Arranger has represented, warranted and undertaken that:

(a) it has not made and will not make an offer of Notes to the public in that member state, except to any legal
entity which is a qualified investor as defined in Regulation (EU) 2017/1129 (as amended, the "EU Prospectus
Regulation"), or in any other circumstances falling within Article 1(4) of the EU Prospectus Regulation,
provided that no such offer requires the Issuer or the Arranger to publish a prospectus under Article 3 of the EU
Prospectus Regulation or to supplement a prospectus under Article 23 of the EU Prospectus Regulation;

(b) it has not offered, sold or otherwise made available, and will not offer, sell or otherwise make available,
any Notes to any retail investor in the EEA, as defined in the Important Notice; and

(c) it will not sell any Notes to a retail client in the EEA otherwise than in accordance with Article 3 of
Regulation (EU) 2017/2402 (as amended, the "EU Securitisation Regulation").

For the purposes of this provision, the expression an "offer of Notes to the public" means the communication in
any form and by any means of sufficient information on the terms of the offer and the Notes to be offered so as
to enable an investor to decide to purchase or subscribe for the Notes.

The Transaction has been structured to comply with the UK securitisation framework. Investors established in
the EEA and subject to Article 5 of the EU Securitisation Regulation are responsible for their own assessment of
whether the retention by the Society described in Section 17 and the information made available under Section 20
enable them to comply with their due diligence obligations under that Article. None of the Issuer, the Society
and the Arranger gives any assurance that they do.

### United States

The Notes have not been and will not be registered under the Securities Act and may not be offered, sold or
delivered within the United States or to, or for the account or benefit of, U.S. persons (as defined in
Regulation S under the Securities Act ("Regulation S")), except in certain transactions exempt from, or not
subject to, the registration requirements of the Securities Act. The Arranger has represented, warranted and
undertaken that:

(a) it has offered and sold, and will offer and sell, the Notes only outside the United States to persons who are
not U.S. persons in offshore transactions in reliance on Regulation S, both as part of its distribution at any
time and otherwise until 40 days after the later of the commencement of the offering and the Issue Date (the
"Distribution Compliance Period");

(b) neither it, nor its affiliates, nor any person acting on its or their behalf, has engaged or will engage in
any directed selling efforts with respect to the Notes, and it and they have complied and will comply with the
offering restrictions requirement of Regulation S;

(c) at or before confirmation of the sale of any Notes, it will have sent to each distributor, dealer or person
receiving a selling concession, fee or other remuneration that purchases Notes from it during the Distribution
Compliance Period a confirmation or notice setting out the restrictions on offers and sales of the Notes within
the United States or to, or for the account or benefit of, U.S. persons; and

(d) it will not sell any Notes to, or for the account or benefit of, any "U.S. person" as defined in the credit
risk retention regulations issued under Section 15G of the U.S. Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the
"U.S. Risk Retention Rules"), except with the prior written consent of the Society.

The Transaction is not intended to involve the retention by a sponsor of a credit risk for the purposes of the
U.S. Risk Retention Rules. The Issuer intends to rely on the exemption for non-U.S. transactions in Section 20 of
the U.S. Risk Retention Rules, one of whose conditions is that no more than 10 per cent of the dollar value (or
its equivalent) of all classes of interests issued in the securitisation are sold or transferred to, or for the
account or benefit of, U.S. persons for the purposes of those rules. Each purchaser of Notes, including a
purchaser of beneficial interests in the Notes, will be deemed to represent that it is not such a U.S. person,
or that it has obtained the consent of the Society. The Issuer has not been and will not be registered under the
U.S. Investment Company Act of 1940, as amended. Terms used in this paragraph have the meanings given to them by
Regulation S.

### Prohibition of Sales to Retail Investors

The Notes are offered only to professional investors and eligible counterparties. Each purchaser of Notes, in the
initial distribution and in any subsequent resale, will be deemed to represent and agree that it is not a retail
investor in the United Kingdom or the EEA, as defined in the Important Notice, and that it will not offer, sell
or otherwise make available any Note to such a person. Any Distributor reselling the Notes is responsible for its
own compliance with the product governance rules and with the restrictions on retail distribution that apply to
it, including the restrictions on the sale of securitisation positions to retail clients.

### Other Jurisdictions

No action has been taken to permit an offer of the Notes in any jurisdiction other than the United Kingdom and
the member states of the EEA, and then only to the persons and in the circumstances described in this Section.
The Arranger has undertaken that it will not offer or sell Notes, or distribute this Offering Circular, in any
other jurisdiction except in circumstances that will result in compliance with the applicable laws and
regulations of that jurisdiction and that will not impose any obligation on the Issuer or the Society.

### Secondary Market and Other Relationships

The selling restrictions in this Section may be modified by agreement between the Issuer and the Arranger
following a change in a relevant law, regulation or directive, or in its interpretation. Each person who acquires
Notes in the secondary market will be deemed to have made the representations and undertakings in this Section
that apply to it, as if references to the Arranger were references to that person.

The Arranger and its affiliates have engaged, and may in the future engage, in investment banking, commercial
banking and other transactions with the Society and its affiliates in the ordinary course of business, for which
they have received and may receive customary fees. Halcyon Bank plc, in addition to its role as arranger, acts as
Account Bank, Collection Account Bank, Swap Provider, Principal Paying Agent, Agent Bank and Registrar,
as described in Section 2, and its interests in those capacities may differ from those of the Noteholders. The
Arranger is under no obligation to make a market in the Notes, and it may purchase, hold and sell Notes for its
own account or for the accounts of its customers.
