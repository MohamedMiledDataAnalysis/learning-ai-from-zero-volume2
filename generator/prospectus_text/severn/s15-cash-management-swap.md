## Section 15 — Cash Management, Accounts and the Swap {-}

This Section 15 describes the flow of funds from the borrowers to the Issuer, the bank accounts of the Issuer,
the ledgers maintained by the Cash Manager, the interest rate swap entered into by the Issuer and the rating
requirements that apply to the banks and the swap counterparty of the Issuer. It summarises the Cash Management
Agreement, the Collection Account Declaration of Trust, the Bank Account Agreement and the Swap Agreement
(each as defined below), and is qualified by the full terms of those documents, copies of which are available
for inspection as described in Section 20. The orders in which the Issuer's funds are applied on each
Interest Payment Date are set out in Section 6 (Revenue Priority of Payments) and Section 7 (Principal
Priority of Payments), and are not repeated here.

### The Cash Manager

Marlowe Building Society (the "Cash Manager") will act as cash manager of the Issuer under the Cash Management
Agreement for the fee set out in Section 14. The Cash Manager will, on behalf of the Issuer and, after the
delivery of an enforcement notice, the Security Trustee:

(a) operate the Issuer Accounts and make the transfers between them required by the Transaction Documents;

(b) maintain the ledgers described below and record in them every receipt and payment of the Issuer;

(c) on each Calculation Date, determine the Available Revenue Receipts and the Available Principal Receipts for
the Collection Period ending on that Calculation Date, calculate the amounts payable on the following
Interest Payment Date, and determine whether a Sequential Payment Trigger has occurred, by calculating the
Arrears Ratio, the Cumulative Loss Ratio and the ratio relevant to the Reserve Trigger described in Section 8;

(d) on each Interest Payment Date, apply the Available Revenue Receipts and the Available Principal Receipts in
the orders set out in Sections 6 and 7, and make the drawings on the Liquidity Reserve Fund and the General
Reserve Fund described in Section 6;

(e) notify the Issuer, the Note Trustee and the Rating Agencies of the amounts of interest and principal
payable on each class of Notes, for publication to the Noteholders in accordance with the Conditions;

(f) invest funds standing to the credit of the Issuer Accounts in Authorised Investments, as described below;

(g) prepare the Investor Report described in Section 14 and the reports required by the Securitisation
Regulations described in Section 17; and

(h) make the calculations and payments due under the Swap Agreement.

A "Collection Period" is each period from (but excluding) a Calculation Date to (and including) the next
Calculation Date, except that the first Collection Period runs from (but excluding) the Portfolio Reference
Date, [[reference_date]], to (and including) the Calculation Date immediately before the First Interest
Payment Date. All amounts received on the Mortgage Loans after the Portfolio Reference Date belong to the
Issuer.

If the Cash Manager has not received a Monthly Mortgage Administration Report for any month of a Collection
Period by the relevant Calculation Date, it will estimate the Available Revenue Receipts and the Available
Principal Receipts for that month on the basis of the three most recent Monthly Mortgage Administration Reports
it has received. When the missing report is received, the Cash Manager will reconcile the estimate with the
actual receipts and make the corresponding adjustments on the next Interest Payment Date.

The appointment of the Cash Manager may be terminated by the Issuer or the Security Trustee on the occurrence
of events equivalent to the Mortgage Administrator Termination Events described in Section 14. The Cash
Manager may resign on not less than twelve months' notice. No termination or resignation will take effect
until a substitute cash manager, experienced in the cash management of securitisation transactions, has been
appointed on substantially the same terms. If the long-term ratings of the Society fall below the levels
specified in the Cash Management Agreement, the Issuer, with the assistance of the Corporate Services Provider,
will use reasonable endeavours to appoint a back-up cash manager.

### Revenue and principal receipts

The "Available Revenue Receipts" for each Interest Payment Date will be, in summary, the aggregate of:

(a) the revenue receipts on the Mortgage Loans received in the Collection Period, being payments of interest,
fees, early repayment charges and other amounts that are not principal, and recoveries received after the
sale of a property in possession;

(b) the part of the repurchase price of any Mortgage Loan repurchased by the Seller in the Collection Period
that represents accrued interest;

(c) interest received on the Issuer Accounts and income from Authorised Investments;

(d) the net amounts received by the Issuer under the Swap Agreement, other than termination payments
applied to the cost of a replacement swap and amounts of collateral;

(e) the amounts drawn from the Liquidity Reserve Fund and the General Reserve Fund, in the circumstances
described in Section 6;

(f) any Available Principal Receipts applied to a revenue shortfall under item (a) of the Principal Priority of
Payments in Section 7; and

(g) any excess Available Principal Receipts transferred under item (g) of the Principal Priority of Payments.

The "Available Principal Receipts" for each Interest Payment Date will be, in summary, the aggregate of:

(a) the principal receipts on the Mortgage Loans received in the Collection Period, including scheduled
repayments, overpayments, redemptions and the proceeds of enforcement applied to principal;

(b) the part of the repurchase price of any Mortgage Loan repurchased by the Seller in the Collection Period
that represents principal; and

(c) the amounts credited to the Principal Deficiency Ledger from Available Revenue Receipts on that Interest
Payment Date, in accordance with Section 6.

### The Collection Account and the Collection Account Declaration of Trust

The borrowers pay their monthly instalments into an account in the name of the Society (the "Collection
Account") held with Halcyon Bank plc (the "Collection Account Bank"). The Collection Account receives payments on the
Mortgage Loans and also on other mortgage loans of the Society that have not been sold to the Issuer, including
the mortgage loans comprised in the retained exposures described in Section 17.

Because the Collection Account receives the Society's own funds as well as those of the Issuer, the Society
will, on the Issue Date, declare a trust over the Collection Account (the "Collection Account Declaration of
Trust"), under which it will hold the amounts standing to the credit of the Collection Account that derive from
the Mortgage Loans on trust for the Issuer, and the remaining amounts on trust for itself and for the
beneficiaries of other declarations of trust over the Collection Account, if any. The Collection Account
Declaration of Trust is intended to ensure that the Issuer's share of the amounts in the Collection Account
does not form part of the Society's estate if the Society becomes insolvent. The amounts paid into the
Collection Account in respect of the Scottish Mortgage Loans are held on trust for the Issuer under the same
declaration.

The Mortgage Administrator will transfer the amounts received in the Collection Account that derive from the
Mortgage Loans to the Transaction Account no later than the Business Day following the day on which they are
identified as cleared funds. The Mortgage Administrator may not use the amounts held on trust for the Issuer
for any other purpose. If a direct debit is reversed after its amount has been transferred to the Transaction
Account, the Mortgage Administrator may deduct the reversed amount from subsequent transfers.

If the Collection Account Bank ceases to have the ratings specified in the Collection Account Declaration of
Trust, the Society will transfer the Collection Account to a bank with those ratings within thirty days, or
take such other action as the Rating Agencies confirm will not adversely affect the ratings of the Notes. A
risk remains that, during the period between the receipt of an amount in the Collection Account and its
transfer to the Transaction Account, the amount may be affected by the insolvency of the Society or of the
Collection Account Bank. The risk is described in Section 1.

### The Issuer Accounts

The Issuer will maintain the following accounts with Halcyon Bank plc (the "Account Bank") under a bank
account agreement between the Issuer, the Account Bank, the Cash Manager and the Security Trustee (the
"Bank Account Agreement") (together, the "Issuer Accounts"):

(a) the transaction account (the "Transaction Account"), into which the Mortgage Administrator transfers the
receipts on the Mortgage Loans, the Swap Provider pays the net amounts due to the Issuer under the Swap
Agreement, and from which the Cash Manager makes the payments on each Interest Payment Date;

(b) the general reserve account (the "General Reserve Account"), to which the General Reserve Fund is credited;

(c) the liquidity reserve account (the "Liquidity Reserve Account"), to which the Liquidity Reserve Fund is
credited; and

(d) the swap collateral account (the "Swap Collateral Account"), to which any collateral transferred by the
Swap Provider under the Swap Agreement is credited.

Each of the Issuer Accounts is charged by the Issuer in favour of the Security Trustee under the Deed of
Charge. The Account Bank pays interest on the balances of the Issuer Accounts at the rate agreed in the
Bank Account Agreement, which may be lower than the rate of interest on the Notes. No account may be
overdrawn.

The Cash Manager may invest amounts standing to the credit of the Transaction Account, the General Reserve
Account and the Liquidity Reserve Account in sterling demand or time deposits, certificates of deposit and
short-term debt obligations that meet the rating requirements of the Rating Agencies and mature no later than
the Business Day before the next Interest Payment Date (the "Authorised Investments"). Authorised Investments
must not be securitisation positions. Amounts in the Swap Collateral Account may not be invested except as
the Swap Agreement permits.

### The Issuer ledgers

The Cash Manager will maintain the following ledgers in the books of the Issuer, which record the amounts held
by the Issuer for each purpose; they are not separate bank accounts.

(a) The "Revenue Ledger" records the revenue receipts of the Issuer and their application in accordance with
the Revenue Priority of Payments.

(b) The "Principal Ledger" records the principal receipts of the Issuer and their application in accordance
with the Principal Priority of Payments.

(c) The "Principal Deficiency Ledger", which comprises one sub-ledger for each class of Notes, records the
Realised Losses on the Mortgage Loans and the Available Principal Receipts applied to pay a revenue shortfall,
as described in Section 5. Debits are recorded on the Class Z sub-ledger first and then on each more senior
sub-ledger in turn, up to the principal amount outstanding of the relevant class of Notes, and are cleared by
credits from Available Revenue Receipts in the order set out in Section 6.

(d) The "General Reserve Ledger" and the "Liquidity Reserve Ledger" record the amounts of the General Reserve
Fund and the Liquidity Reserve Fund, their drawings and their replenishment.

(e) The "Swap Collateral Ledger" records the collateral received from, and returned to, the Swap Provider.

### The reserve funds

The Issuer will establish two reserve funds on the Issue Date: the General Reserve Fund, held in the General
Reserve Account, and the Liquidity Reserve Fund, held in the Liquidity Reserve Account. Both are funded on the
Issue Date from the proceeds of the Subordinated Loan made by the Seller. Their required amounts, their uses
and the classes of Notes they support are set out in Section 5, and the order in which they are drawn and
replenished is set out in Section 6. The amounts drawn from either reserve fund on an Interest Payment Date
form part of the Available Revenue Receipts for that Interest Payment Date and are recorded as a debit on the
General Reserve Ledger or the Liquidity Reserve Ledger. The level of the General Reserve Fund is also relevant
to the Reserve Trigger described in Section 8.

### The interest rate swap

**Purpose.** The Fixed Rate Loans bear interest at fixed rates for their Fixed Rate Period, while the Class A,
Class B, Class C and Class D Notes bear interest at Compounded Daily SONIA plus a margin. If Compounded Daily
SONIA rose, the interest received on the Fixed Rate Loans would not rise with it, and the Issuer might not have
enough revenue to pay interest on the Notes. To hedge that risk, the Issuer will enter into an interest rate
swap transaction (the "Swap") with Halcyon Bank plc (the "Swap Provider") under an ISDA master agreement, a
schedule, a credit support annex and a confirmation (together, the "Swap Agreement"). The Swap Agreement is
governed by English law.

**Mechanics.** On each Interest Payment Date:

(a) the Issuer will pay to the Swap Provider an amount equal to a fixed rate, set on the Issue Date, applied to
the Notional Amount for the relevant calculation period; and

(b) the Swap Provider will pay to the Issuer an amount equal to Compounded Daily SONIA for that calculation
period applied to the same Notional Amount.

The two amounts are netted, and only the net amount is paid, by the party owing the larger amount. A net amount
due to the Swap Provider ranks at item (c) of the Revenue Priority of Payments in Section 6, ahead of interest on
the Notes. A net amount due to the Issuer forms part of the Available Revenue Receipts.

The "Notional Amount" for each calculation period is the lower of (a) the amount for that period set out in a
schedule to the confirmation, which was calculated on the Issue Date on the assumption that the Fixed Rate
Loans amortise in accordance with their terms, are not in default and prepay at a constant rate, and which
reduces to zero as each Fixed Rate Loan reaches the end of its Fixed Rate Period, and (b) the aggregate current
balance of the Mortgage Loans that are Fixed Rate Loans in their original Fixed Rate Period at the start of the
period. The Swap therefore hedges only the original Fixed Rate Period of the Fixed Rate Loans in the Provisional
Portfolio, as stated in Section 1. It does not hedge the Tracker Loans, the Mortgage Loans at the Standard
Variable Rate, or Mortgage Loans that become Fixed Rate Loans by a Product Switch after the Issue Date. The
mismatch between the Bank Rate and the Standard Variable Rate, on the one hand, and Compounded Daily SONIA, on
the other, is not hedged, and the resulting basis risk is described in Section 1.

**Termination.** The Swap Agreement may be terminated early on the occurrence of an event of default or a
termination event under it, which include: the failure of either party to make a payment when due, subject to a
grace period; the insolvency of either party; the delivery of an enforcement notice in relation to the Notes;
the redemption of all of the Notes; a change in tax law that requires a party to deduct tax from its payments;
and the failure of the Swap Provider to take the action required on a downgrade described below. On early
termination, a termination payment may be due from either party, calculated on the basis of market quotations
for a replacement transaction or of the loss of the non-defaulting party.

A termination payment due from the Issuer ranks at item (c) of the Revenue Priority of Payments, unless the
Swap Agreement was terminated because the Swap Provider was the defaulting party or because of the Swap
Provider's failure to take the action required on a downgrade, in which case it ranks at item (o) as a
subordinated swap termination payment. Any premium received by the Issuer from a replacement swap provider will
be applied first to pay any termination payment due to the outgoing Swap Provider, and any termination payment
received by the Issuer will be applied first to the cost of entering into a replacement swap. Neither amount
forms part of the Available Revenue Receipts except to the extent that it exceeds the amount so applied.

**Tax.** If the Swap Provider is required by law to deduct tax from its payments, it must gross up its payments
so that the Issuer receives the amount it would have received without the deduction. The Issuer is not required
to gross up its payments to the Swap Provider.

### Rating triggers: the Swap Provider

The Swap Agreement contains rating triggers set by reference to the criteria of the Rating Agencies. If the
ratings of the Swap Provider, or of any guarantor of its obligations, fall below the first level of ratings
specified in the Swap Agreement (the "Initial Required Ratings"), the Swap Provider must, at its own cost,
within the period set out in the Swap Agreement (normally fourteen days), transfer collateral to the Swap
Collateral Account in accordance with the credit support annex, or transfer its obligations to an entity with
the Initial Required Ratings, or obtain a guarantee from such an entity.

If the ratings of the Swap Provider fall below a second and lower level of ratings specified in the Swap Agreement
(the "Subsequent Required Ratings"), the Swap Provider must, at its own cost, within the period set out in the
Swap Agreement (normally thirty days), transfer its obligations to an entity with the required ratings or obtain
a guarantee from such an entity, and must in the meantime continue to transfer collateral, in a greater amount,
under the credit support annex. If the Swap Provider fails to take the required action within the required
period, an additional termination event will occur, and the Issuer may terminate the Swap Agreement.

Collateral transferred by the Swap Provider is held in the Swap Collateral Account and recorded on the Swap
Collateral Ledger. It does not form part of the Available Revenue Receipts or the Available Principal Receipts,
and is not applied in the Revenue Priority of Payments. It may be applied only to satisfy the obligations of the
Swap Provider under the Swap Agreement on its termination, or returned to the Swap Provider, in accordance with
the credit support annex.

### Rating triggers: the Account Bank

If the Account Bank ceases to have the ratings specified in the Bank Account Agreement by reference to the
criteria of the Rating Agencies (the "Account Bank Required Ratings"), the Cash Manager, on behalf of the Issuer,
must, within thirty calendar days of the downgrade (or such longer period as the Rating Agencies confirm will not
adversely affect the ratings of the Notes):

(a) close the Issuer Accounts and open replacement accounts on substantially the same terms with a bank that has
the Account Bank Required Ratings, and transfer to them the balances of the Issuer Accounts; or

(b) obtain a guarantee of the obligations of the Account Bank from a bank that has the Account Bank Required
Ratings; or

(c) take such other action as the Rating Agencies confirm will not adversely affect the ratings of the Notes.

Authorised Investments held with or issued by the Account Bank must be realised or transferred on the same
basis. The costs of the replacement are borne by the outgoing Account Bank, to the extent set out in the
Bank Account Agreement.

### Conflicts of interest of Halcyon Bank plc

Halcyon Bank plc acts in this transaction as the Arranger, the Account Bank, the Collection Account Bank and
the Swap Provider. In each of these roles it acts in its own interest and under the terms of the relevant
Transaction Document, and it owes no fiduciary duty to the Noteholders. Its rights as Swap Provider, including its
right to a termination payment ranking ahead of interest on the Notes, may conflict with the interests of the
Noteholders. A downgrade of Halcyon Bank plc would affect its roles as Account Bank, Collection Account Bank
and Swap Provider at the same time, and the rating triggers described in this Section 15 would then require the
replacement of more than one counterparty within a short period. The risks arising from these concentrations are
described in Section 1.
