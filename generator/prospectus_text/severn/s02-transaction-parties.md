this Offering Circular (the "Transaction") and the capacities in which it acts. Each party is described in more
detail below. The agreements under which the parties act (together, the "Transaction Documents") are listed in
Section 20, where they are stated to be available for inspection.

### Severn Residential Mortgages 2026-1 plc

Severn Residential Mortgages 2026-1 plc (the "Issuer") is a public limited company incorporated in England and
Wales. It is a special purpose vehicle whose only business is to issue the Notes, to acquire the Mortgage Loans
and their Related Security from the Seller, and to enter into and perform the Transaction Documents. The Issuer
has no employees and no subsidiaries. Its incorporation, share capital, directors and capitalisation are
described in Section 9.

### Holdings

Severn Residential Mortgages Holdings Limited ("Holdings") is a private limited company incorporated in England
and Wales which holds the entire beneficial interest in the issued share capital of the Issuer. The issued
share capital of Holdings is held by Pellam Corporate Services Ltd (the "Share Trustee") on a discretionary
trust for charitable purposes. Neither the Seller nor any member of its group has any direct or indirect
ownership interest in Holdings or in the Issuer. Holdings is described in Section 9.

### Marlowe Building Society

Marlowe Building Society (the "Society") is a building society incorporated in England under the Building
Societies Act 1986, authorised by the Prudential Regulation Authority (the "PRA") and regulated by the FCA and
the PRA. The Society was founded in Worcester in 1871 and has approximately 712,000 members and 63 branches. It
originated each of the Mortgage Loans in the Provisional Portfolio.

Marlowe Building Society (the "Seller") will sell the Mortgage Loans and their Related Security to the Issuer on
the Issue Date under the Mortgage Sale Agreement and will give the Loan Warranties described in Section 12. The
sale of the English and Welsh Mortgage Loans will take effect in equity, and the sale of the Scottish Mortgage
Loans will take effect by means of the Scottish Declaration of Trust, as described in Section 10.

Marlowe Building Society (the "Mortgage Administrator") will administer the Mortgage Loans on behalf of the
Issuer under the Mortgage Administration Agreement, as described in Section 14, and Marlowe Building Society
(the "Cash Manager") will operate the Issuer Accounts, maintain the ledgers of the Issuer and apply the
Available Revenue Receipts and the Available Principal Receipts under the Cash Management Agreement, as
described in Section 15.

Marlowe Building Society (the "Subordinated Loan Provider") will advance to the Issuer on the Issue Date a
subordinated loan (the "Subordinated Loan") under a subordinated loan agreement (the "Subordinated Loan
Agreement"), the proceeds of which will fund the General Reserve Fund and the Liquidity Reserve Fund as
described in Section 5. The Subordinated Loan ranks below all payments on the Notes in the Revenue Priority of
Payments. The Society will also retain a material net economic interest in the Transaction, as described in Section 17.

### The Note Trustee and the Security Trustee

The Note Trustee will act as trustee for the holders of the Notes (the "Noteholders") under the trust deed
constituting the Notes (the "Trust Deed"). The Security Trustee will hold the security created by the Issuer
under the deed of charge dated on or about the Issue Date (the "Deed of Charge") on trust for the Noteholders
and the other persons to whom the Issuer owes obligations under the Transaction Documents (together, the
"Secured Creditors"). By the Deed of Charge, the Issuer assigns by way of first fixed security its interest in
the English and Welsh Mortgage Loans, its beneficial interest under the Scottish Declaration of Trust (by way of
an assignation in security governed by Scots law), its rights under the Transaction Documents and the Issuer
Accounts, and grants a first floating charge over the whole of its undertaking and assets.

The Note Trustee and the Security Trustee are the same trust corporation, which is incorporated in England and
Wales. Their fees and expenses rank first in the Revenue Priority of Payments, as set out in Section 6. Neither
the Note Trustee nor the Security Trustee is under any obligation to monitor the performance of the Mortgage
Loans or of any party to the Transaction Documents, and each is entitled to be indemnified and secured to its
satisfaction before taking any action. Their powers, their discretions and the circumstances in which they
may retire or be removed are set out in the Conditions in Section 4.

### Halcyon Bank plc

Halcyon Bank plc is a public limited company incorporated in England and Wales, authorised by the PRA and
regulated by the FCA and the PRA. It acts in several capacities.

Halcyon Bank plc (the "Arranger") structured the Transaction and has agreed, under the Subscription Agreement
described in Section 19, to subscribe or procure subscribers for the Notes, other than any Notes acquired by the
Seller.

Halcyon Bank plc (the "Account Bank") will hold the transaction account and the reserve accounts of the
Issuer (the "Issuer Accounts") under the bank account agreement dated on or about the Issue Date (the "Bank
Account Agreement"). If the Account Bank ceases to have the minimum ratings required by the Rating
Agencies, the Issuer Accounts will be transferred to a suitably rated bank, as described in Section 15.

Halcyon Bank plc (the "Collection Account Bank") holds the account of the Society into which borrowers' monthly
payments are collected (the "Collection Account"). The Society holds the amounts credited to the Collection
Account in respect of the Mortgage Loans on trust for the Issuer under a declaration of trust (the "Collection
Account Declaration of Trust"), and those amounts are transferred to the transaction account of the Issuer as
described in Section 15.

Halcyon Bank plc (the "Swap Provider") will enter into an interest rate swap with the Issuer (the "Swap
Agreement") to hedge the difference between the fixed rates payable on the fixed-rate Mortgage Loans and
Compounded Daily SONIA payable on the Rated Notes, as described in Section 15. Net amounts due to the Swap
Provider rank in the Revenue Priority of Payments as set out in Section 6, and certain termination payments are
subordinated.

Halcyon Bank plc (the "Principal Paying Agent"), Halcyon Bank plc (the "Agent Bank") and Halcyon Bank plc (the
"Registrar") will act under the agency agreement dated on or about the Issue Date (the "Agency Agreement"): the
Agent Bank determines the rate of interest on each Class of Rated Notes for each Interest Period, the Principal
Paying Agent makes payments on the Notes to the Clearing Systems, and the Registrar maintains the register of
the Noteholders.

### Pellam Corporate Services Ltd

Pellam Corporate Services Ltd (the "Corporate Services Provider") is a private limited company incorporated in
England and Wales that provides directors, company secretarial and administrative services to special purpose
companies. Under the corporate services agreement dated on or about the Issue Date (the "Corporate Services
Agreement"), it will provide the directors and the company secretary of the Issuer and of Holdings. If a
Mortgage Administrator Termination Event occurs, the Corporate Services Provider will assist the Issuer in
appointing a back-up mortgage administrator, as described in Section 14. No back-up mortgage administrator is
appointed on the Issue Date.

### Legal Advisers, Auditors and Rating Agencies

Grantley Moss LLP is legal adviser to the Arranger, the Note Trustee and the Security Trustee as to English law,
and Hartwell Quinn LLP is legal adviser to the Issuer and the Seller as to English law, Scots law and United
Kingdom taxation. Fennick & Rowe LLP, chartered accountants and registered auditors, have been appointed as the
auditors of the Issuer and of Holdings (the "Auditors"). The Rating Agencies are each established in the United
Kingdom and registered under the UK CRA Regulation; they are not parties to the Transaction Documents.

### Conflicts of Interest

The Society acts in five capacities (seller, mortgage administrator, cash manager, subordinated loan provider
and retention holder), and Halcyon Bank plc acts in seven capacities. Each acts in each capacity only in accordance with the
Transaction Documents to which it is a party in that capacity and may have interests which differ from those of
the Noteholders. The risks arising from these arrangements are described in Section 1.
