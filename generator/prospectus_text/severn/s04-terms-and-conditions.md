### Condition 1 — Form, Denomination and Title

**1.1 Form.** Each Class of Notes will be issued in fully registered form, without interest coupons. Each Class
will initially be represented by a single global note certificate in registered form (each, a "Global Note"),
registered in the name of a nominee of, and deposited with, a common safekeeper for the international central
securities depositaries through which the Notes are cleared (the "Clearing Systems"). The Notes will not be
listed or admitted to trading on any stock exchange or market.

**1.2 Denomination.** The Notes will be issued in minimum denominations of £100,000 and integral multiples of
£1,000 in excess thereof (each, an "Authorised Denomination"). Interests in a Global Note may be held and
transferred only in Authorised Denominations.

**1.3 Definitive Notes.** Interests in a Global Note will be exchanged for individual note certificates in
registered form (the "Definitive Notes") only if:

(a) the Clearing Systems are closed for business for a continuous period of 14 days (other than by reason of a
    holiday, statutory or otherwise) or announce an intention permanently to cease business, and no
    successor clearing system satisfactory to the Note Trustee is available; or
(b) as a result of any change in, or amendment to, the laws or regulations of the United Kingdom, or of any
    change in their application or official interpretation, which becomes effective on or after the Issue
    Date, the Issuer or any Agent is or will be required to make any deduction or withholding for or on account
    of tax from any payment on the Notes that would not be required if the Notes were in definitive form.

The Issuer will deliver the Definitive Notes within 30 days of the relevant event, in Authorised Denominations
only, and a Noteholder holding less than £100,000 in the Clearing Systems at the time will not be entitled to a
Definitive Note for that holding.

**1.4 Global Notes.** While the Notes are represented by a Global Note: (a) payments will be made to the
registered holder of the Global Note in accordance with Condition 7; (b) notices may be given through the
Clearing Systems in accordance with Condition 14; (c) the registered holder of the Global Note will be treated
as having one vote for each £1,000 of Principal Amount Outstanding represented by it; and (d) each person who
is shown in the records of a Clearing System as entitled to a principal amount of Notes (an "Accountholder")

**1.5 Title.** Title to the Notes will pass only by registration in the register maintained by the Registrar (the

**1.6 Transfers.** A Definitive Note may be transferred, in whole or in an Authorised Denomination, by surrender

### Condition 2 — Status and Ranking

**2.1 Status.** The Notes are direct, secured, limited recourse and unconditional obligations of the Issuer. They
are secured by the Security described in Condition 3, and payments on them are limited as described in
Condition 11.

**2.2 Ranking within each Class.** The Notes of each Class rank *pari passu* and without any preference among
themselves.

**2.3 Ranking between Classes before enforcement.** Before the delivery of an Enforcement Notice:

(a) interest on the Rated Notes is paid out of Available Revenue Receipts (and, where applicable, drawings on the
    Liquidity Reserve Fund and the General Reserve Fund) in the order set out in the Revenue Priority of
    Payments in Section 6, so that interest on the Class A Notes ranks ahead of interest on the Class B Notes,
    which ranks ahead of interest on the Class C Notes, which ranks ahead of interest on the Class D Notes;
(b) principal on the Notes is paid out of Available Principal Receipts in the order set out in the Principal
    Priority of Payments in Section 7, sequentially or, where the conditions described in Section 7 are
    satisfied and no Sequential Payment Trigger has occurred, *pro rata* between the Class A Notes, the Class B
    Notes, the Class C Notes and the Class D Notes, with principal on the Class Z Notes paid only after the
    Rated Notes have been redeemed in full; and
(c) the debit balances on the Principal Deficiency Ledgers are cleared from Available Revenue Receipts at the
    positions in the Revenue Priority of Payments set out in Section 6.

**2.4 Ranking between Classes after enforcement.** After the delivery of an Enforcement Notice, the Class A Notes
rank in priority to the Class B Notes, the Class B Notes rank in priority to the Class C Notes, the Class C
Notes rank in priority to the Class D Notes and the Class D Notes rank in priority to the Class Z Notes, in
each case as to payments of both interest and principal, in accordance with the Post-Enforcement Priority of
Payments described in Condition 11.4.

**2.5 Class Z Notes.** The Class Z Notes do not bear interest. They are the first Class to absorb losses on the
Portfolio, through the Class Z Principal Deficiency Ledger, and they are not covered by the General Reserve Fund
or the Liquidity Reserve Fund. Their return to the holder consists only of the repayment of their Principal
Amount Outstanding to the extent that funds are available for that purpose in the Principal Priority of
Payments or, after enforcement, in the Post-Enforcement Priority of Payments.

**2.6 Conflicts of interest.** The Trust Deed requires the Note Trustee, in the exercise of its powers, trusts,
authorities, duties and discretions, to have regard to the interests of each Class as a class, and not to the
consequences for individual Noteholders. If there is, in the opinion of the Note Trustee, a conflict between the
interests of the holders of two or more Classes, the Note Trustee is required to have regard only to the
interests of the holders of the Most Senior Class. If there is a conflict between the interests of the
Noteholders and those of the other Secured Creditors, the Security Trustee is required to have regard only to
the interests of the Noteholders (and, among them, to those of the Most Senior Class), save that the
Security Trustee will have regard to the interests of the Swap Provider in relation to the rights of the Swap
Provider over the Swap Collateral.

### Condition 5 — Interest

**5.1 Accrual.** Each Rated Note bears interest on its Principal Amount Outstanding from (and including) the Issue
Date. Interest is payable quarterly in arrear on each Interest Payment Date for the Interest Period ending on
(but excluding) that Interest Payment Date. The Class Z Notes do not bear interest, and no amount is payable on
them under this Condition. Each Rated Note will cease to bear interest from the date on which it is redeemed in
full, unless payment of principal is improperly withheld or refused, in which case interest will continue to
accrue (after as well as before judgment) at the rate determined under this Condition until the day on which all
sums due on that Note are received by or on behalf of the relevant Noteholder.

**5.2 Rate of Interest.** The rate of interest payable on each Class of Rated Notes for each Interest Period (the
"Rate of Interest") is the aggregate of Compounded Daily SONIA for that Interest Period and the Margin of that
Class. The "Margin" of each Class is:

(a) for each Interest Period ending on or before the Step-Up Date, the margin for that Class shown in the column
    "Margin to Step-Up Date" in Section 3; and
(b) for each Interest Period beginning on or after the Step-Up Date, the margin for that Class shown in the
    column "Margin after Step-Up Date" in Section 3.

If the Rate of Interest of a Class for an Interest Period would be less than zero, it will be zero.

**5.3 Compounded Daily SONIA.** "Compounded Daily SONIA" means, for an Interest Period, the rate of return of a
daily compound interest investment in sterling, with the daily Sterling Overnight Index Average as reference
rate, calculated by the Agent Bank on the Interest Determination Date for that Interest Period by the following
method, the resulting percentage being rounded, if necessary, to the fourth decimal place, with 0.00005 being
rounded upwards:

(a) for each London Banking Day in the Observation Period, the Agent Bank multiplies the SONIA rate for that
    London Banking Day by the number of calendar days from (and including) that London Banking Day to (but
    excluding) the next London Banking Day, divides the result by 365 and adds one;
(b) the Agent Bank multiplies together the factors obtained under paragraph (a) for all the London Banking Days
    in the Observation Period and subtracts one from the product; and
(c) the Agent Bank multiplies the result by 365 and divides it by the number of calendar days in the Observation
    Period.

For this purpose: "**SONIA**" means, for any London Banking Day, the Sterling Overnight Index Average rate for
that day as provided by the Bank of England, as administrator of that rate, or by any successor administrator,
to authorised distributors and as then published on the relevant screen page (the "Relevant Screen Page") or,
if that page is unavailable, as otherwise published by those authorised distributors, in each case on the next
London Banking Day; "**London Banking Day**" means any day on which commercial banks are open for general
business (including dealing in foreign exchange and foreign currency deposits) in London; "**Observation
Period**" means, for an Interest Period, the period from (and including) the date falling five London Banking
Days before the first day of that Interest Period to (but excluding) the date falling five London Banking Days
before the Interest Payment Date at the end of it; and "**Interest Determination Date**" means, for an Interest
Period, the fifth London Banking Day before the Interest Payment Date at the end of it.

Under this method, which shifts both the SONIA rates and their weighting to the Observation Period, each Interest
Period is matched with an Observation Period of equal length that ends five London Banking Days before the
Interest Payment Date, so that the Rate of Interest is known before the payment is made.

**5.4 Fallback.** If, for any London Banking Day in an Observation Period, SONIA is not made available on the
Relevant Screen Page and has not otherwise been published by the authorised distributors, SONIA for that day
will be:

(a) the Bank Rate of the Bank of England (the "Bank Rate") prevailing at close of business on that London
    Banking Day; plus
(b) the mean of the spread of SONIA to the Bank Rate over the previous five days on which SONIA has been
    published, excluding the highest spread (or, if there is more than one highest spread, one only of those
    highest spreads) and the lowest spread (or, if there is more than one lowest spread, one only of those
    lowest spreads).

If the Bank of England publishes guidance as to how SONIA is to be determined, or as to any rate that is to
replace SONIA, the Agent Bank will, to the extent reasonably practicable, follow that guidance for so long as
SONIA is not available. If the Rate of Interest cannot be determined in accordance with Conditions 5.3 and 5.4,
it will be the Rate of Interest last determined for the relevant Class for the preceding Interest Period, with
the Margin applicable to the current Interest Period substituted for the Margin applicable to that preceding
Interest Period (or, for the first Interest Period, the rate that would have applied had the Notes been in issue
for a period of equal length ending on the Issue Date). This Condition is without prejudice to the
modification of the Conditions on a Benchmark Rate Disruption Event under Condition 12.7.

**5.5 Interest Amount.** The Agent Bank will, as soon as practicable after the Interest Determination Date,
calculate the amount of interest payable on each Class of Rated Notes for the Interest Period (the "Interest
Amount") by applying the Rate of Interest to the Principal Amount Outstanding of that Class on the first day of
the Interest Period (after any payment of principal on that day), multiplying the product by the actual number
of days in the Interest Period divided by 365, and rounding the result to the nearest penny (half a penny being
rounded upwards). The Interest Amount payable on each Note of the Class is its rateable share of that amount,
rounded down to the nearest penny.

**5.6 Publication.** The Agent Bank will notify the Rate of Interest and the Interest Amount of each Class of Rated

(a) the amount of the shortfall (the "Deferred Interest") will not fall due on that Interest Payment Date but

(b) the Deferred Interest will itself bear interest at the Rate of Interest of the relevant Class from time to

(c) the Deferred Interest and the interest on it will be paid on the next Interest Payment Date on which, and to

    Priority of Payments; and
(d) all Deferred Interest and interest on it will become due and payable in full on the earlier of the Final

    Enforcement Notice.

The deferral of interest on a Class that is not the Most Senior Class is not an Event of Default.

### Condition 6 — Redemption

**6.1 Final redemption.** Unless previously redeemed in full, the Issuer will redeem each Class of Notes at its
Principal Amount Outstanding on the Interest Payment Date falling on [[final_maturity_date]] (the "Final Maturity

latest final maturity date of any Mortgage Loan permitted by the loan warranties set out in Section 12, so as to
allow for the enforcement of the security for a Mortgage Loan that is in default at its maturity and the sale of
the Property. The Issuer may not redeem the Notes other than in accordance with this Condition and Condition 10.

**6.2 Mandatory redemption.** Before the delivery of an Enforcement Notice, the Issuer will on each Interest
Payment Date apply the Available Principal Receipts in accordance with the Principal Priority of Payments set
out in Section 7, in redemption of the Notes:

(a) sequentially, first in redemption of the Class A Notes until they are redeemed in full, then of the Class B
    Notes, then of the Class C Notes, then of the Class D Notes and finally of the Class Z Notes; or
(b) for so long as no Sequential Payment Trigger has occurred and the other conditions for *pro rata* payment set
    out in Section 7 are satisfied, *pro rata* between the Class A Notes, the Class B Notes, the Class C Notes and
    the Class D Notes, with the Class Z Notes redeemed only after the Rated Notes have been redeemed in full.

The trigger events that give rise to a Sequential Payment Trigger, and the permanence of the switch to sequential
payment, are described in Section 8. The amount applied to each Class is apportioned among the Notes of that
Class *pro rata* to their Principal Amount Outstanding, rounded down to the nearest penny.

**6.3 Principal Deficiency Ledgers.** The Cash Manager will maintain a principal deficiency ledger comprising five
sub-ledgers, one for each Class (the "Class A Principal Deficiency Ledger", the "Class B Principal Deficiency
Ledger", the "Class C Principal Deficiency Ledger", the "Class D Principal Deficiency Ledger" and the "Class Z
Principal Deficiency Ledger", together the "Principal Deficiency Ledgers"). On each Calculation Date:

(a) the Cash Manager will debit to the Principal Deficiency Ledgers the Realised Losses on the Mortgage Loans in
    the Collection Period, and the Available Principal Receipts applied under item (a) of the Principal Priority
    of Payments to pay a revenue shortfall;
(b) each debit will be recorded first on the Class Z Principal Deficiency Ledger, until its balance equals the
    Principal Amount Outstanding of the Class Z Notes, then on the Class D Principal Deficiency Ledger, the
    Class C Principal Deficiency Ledger, the Class B Principal Deficiency Ledger and finally the Class A Principal
    Deficiency Ledger, in each case up to the Principal Amount Outstanding of the relevant Class; and
(c) on the following Interest Payment Date, Available Revenue Receipts (and, for the Class A to D Principal
    Deficiency Ledgers, drawings on the General Reserve Fund) will be credited to the Principal Deficiency
    Ledgers at the positions set out in Section 6, starting with the Class A Principal Deficiency Ledger, and the
    amounts so credited will form part of the Available Principal Receipts.

A debit balance on a Principal Deficiency Ledger is not an Event of Default and does not of itself reduce the
Principal Amount Outstanding of any Note. To the extent that a debit balance has not been cleared by the Final
Maturity Date or on enforcement, the holders of the relevant Class will bear the corresponding loss under
Condition 11.

**6.4 Optional redemption on or after the Step-Up Date.** On the Step-Up Date and on any Interest Payment Date

**6.7 Determinations by the Cash Manager.** On each Calculation Date the Cash Manager will determine the amount of

**6.8 Notice irrevocable; cancellation.** A notice of redemption given under Conditions 6.4 to 6.6 is irrevocable.

owed by the Swap Provider), in the order of priority set out in the Deed of Charge (the "Post-Enforcement Priority

paid first; then the fees of the Mortgage Administrator, the Cash Manager, the Account Bank, the Agents and
the Corporate Services Provider; then amounts due to the Swap Provider other than subordinated swap termination
payments; then interest and principal on the Class A Notes, followed in turn by interest and principal on the
Class B Notes, the Class C Notes and the Class D Notes, with interest on each Class paid before principal on that
Class and nothing paid on a Class until each Class ranking above it has been repaid in full; then principal on the
Class Z Notes; then subordinated swap termination payments; then interest and principal on the Subordinated Loan;
and finally deferred consideration to the Seller.

The Revenue Priority of Payments and the Principal Priority of Payments in Sections 6 and 7 cease to apply on the
delivery of an Enforcement Notice. After enforcement, revenue and principal receipts are no longer separated, the
reserve funds are not replenished and no amount is paid *pro rata* between Classes.

received by it from the Portfolio, the Swap Agreement, the Issuer Accounts and its other rights under the

the Post-Enforcement Priority of Payments, any amount remains unpaid on a Class of Notes, the Security Trustee

### Condition 15 — Substitution of the Issuer

**15.1 Conditions of substitution.** The Note Trustee may, without the consent of the Noteholders, agree to the
substitution of another company (the "Substituted Obligor") in place of the Issuer as principal debtor under the
Notes, the Trust Deed and the other Transaction Documents, provided that:

(a) the Substituted Obligor agrees, in a form satisfactory to the Note Trustee, to be bound by the Notes, the Trust
    Deed and the other Transaction Documents as principal debtor;
(b) the Substituted Obligor acquires the Issuer's interest in the Portfolio, including its beneficial interest
    under the Scottish Declaration of Trust, and grants security in favour of the Security Trustee equivalent to
    the Security, including an equivalent Scottish supplemental charge governed by Scots law;
(c) the Substituted Obligor is a special purpose company subject to restrictions equivalent to those in
    Condition 4;
(d) the Note Trustee is satisfied that the substitution is not materially prejudicial to the interests of the
    holders of the Most Senior Class, and Rating Agency Confirmation has been obtained;
(e) the Swap Provider has consented to the transfer of the Swap Agreement to the Substituted Obligor;
(f) if the Substituted Obligor is resident for tax purposes outside the United Kingdom, undertakings
    corresponding to Condition 8 and Condition 6.6 are given by reference to its territory of residence, and
    legal opinions satisfactory to the Note Trustee are delivered; and
(g) the substitution does not cause the Issuer, the Substituted Obligor or the Seller to breach the UK
    Securitisation Framework.

**15.2 Effect of substitution.** On the substitution taking effect, the Substituted Obligor will be treated as the
Issuer for all purposes of the Notes and the Transaction Documents, and the Issuer will be released from its
obligations. Notice of the substitution will be given to the Noteholders under Condition 14 within 14 days. No
Noteholder may claim any indemnity or payment in respect of the tax consequences of the substitution for that
Noteholder individually.
