## Section 9 — The Issuer and Holdings {-}

### Incorporation and Registered Office

Severn Residential Mortgages 2026-1 plc (the "Issuer") was incorporated in England and Wales on 12 January 2026
as a public company limited by shares under the Companies Act 2006, with registered number 16152408. The
registered office of the Issuer, which is also its principal place of business, is at 12 Cordwainer Yard,
London EC4. The Issuer may be contacted through the Corporate Services Provider at that address.

The Issuer has been established as a special purpose vehicle for the purpose of issuing asset-backed securities.
It is not a subsidiary of the Society and no member of the Society's group has any ownership interest, direct or
indirect, in it. The Issuer intends to be taxed in accordance with the Taxation of Securitisation Companies
Regulations 2006 (SI 2006/3296), as described in Section 18.

### Share Capital

The authorised and issued share capital of the Issuer is £50,000, comprising 50,000 ordinary shares of £1 each,
of which one share is fully paid up and 49,999 shares are each paid up as to 25 pence. Holdings is the legal
and beneficial owner of 49,999 shares, and the remaining share is held by Pellam Corporate Services Ltd as
nominee for Holdings under a declaration of trust in favour of Holdings. The Issuer has no other class of share
capital, and there are no outstanding options, warrants, convertible securities or other rights to subscribe
for its shares.

### Principal Activities

The Issuer's objects are not restricted by its articles of association; its activities are restricted instead
by the covenants given in the Trust Deed and the Deed of Charge. Since its incorporation, the Issuer has not
engaged in any material activities other than: (a) those incidental to its incorporation and to its
registration as a public company under the Companies Act 2006; (b) the authorisation and issue of the Notes;
(c) the matters referred to or contemplated in this Offering Circular; and (d) the authorisation, execution,
delivery and performance of the Transaction Documents to which it is a party. The Issuer has not commenced
operations, and no financial statements have been prepared in respect of it.

From the Issue Date, the activities of the Issuer will comprise: (a) the acquisition and holding of the
Mortgage Loans and their Related Security; (b) the receipt of revenue and principal receipts on the Mortgage
Loans through the Mortgage Administrator and the Collection Account; (c) the payment of interest and principal
on the Notes and of its other obligations in accordance with the Priorities of Payments described in Sections 6
and 7; (d) the performance of its obligations under the Swap Agreement and the Subordinated Loan Agreement; and
(e) the exercise of its rights and the performance of its obligations under the other Transaction Documents.

### Covenants of the Issuer

So long as any Note remains outstanding, the Issuer has covenanted in the Trust Deed and the Deed of Charge
that, save as permitted by the Transaction Documents or with the prior written consent of the Note Trustee, it
will not:

(a) engage in any activity which is not incidental to, or necessary in connection with, the activities
described above;

(b) incur any indebtedness for borrowed money, other than the Notes and the Subordinated Loan, or give any
guarantee or indemnity in respect of any obligation of any person;

(c) create or permit to subsist any security interest over the whole or any part of its assets or undertaking,
other than the security created by the Deed of Charge;

(d) sell, assign, transfer, lend or otherwise dispose of any of its assets, save as provided in the Transaction
Documents;

(e) pay any dividend or make any other distribution to its shareholders, or issue any further shares;

(f) have any employees or premises, or have any subsidiary undertaking;

(g) consolidate or merge with any other person, or convey or transfer its properties or assets substantially as
an entirety to any other person;

(h) have an interest in any bank account other than the Issuer Accounts, save where that account is charged to
the Security Trustee on terms acceptable to it;

(i) amend its articles of association, or agree to any amendment of the Transaction Documents, save in
accordance with the Conditions; or

(j) become resident for tax purposes in any jurisdiction other than the United Kingdom, or have a permanent
establishment outside the United Kingdom.

all or a substantial part of its assets; if it enters into a voluntary arrangement or composition with its
creditors generally; or if an order is made or an effective resolution is passed for its winding-up.

### Holdings

Severn Residential Mortgages Holdings Limited ("Holdings") was incorporated in England and Wales on 9 January
2026 as a private company limited by shares under the Companies Act 2006, with registered number 16148870. Its
registered office is at 12 Cordwainer Yard, London EC4. Its issued share capital is £1, comprising one ordinary
share of £1, fully paid, which is held by the Share Trustee.

The principal objects of Holdings include the acquisition and holding of the shares of the Issuer. Holdings has
not engaged in any activity since its incorporation other than its acquisition of the shares of the Issuer and
activities incidental to it, and it has no employees and no subsidiary other than the Issuer. Holdings has
covenanted with the Security Trustee that, so long as any Note remains outstanding, it will not sell, charge or
otherwise dispose of the shares of the Issuer, and will not permit the Issuer to issue further shares. The
directors and the company secretary of Holdings are the same as those of the Issuer. Holdings does not
guarantee, and has no liability for, any obligation of the Issuer.

### The Share Trust

Pellam Corporate Services Ltd (the "Share Trustee") holds the share of Holdings under a discretionary trust
established by a declaration of trust dated 9 January 2026 (the "Share Trust Deed"), the beneficiaries of which
are charitable purposes. The Share Trustee has no beneficial interest in the share and derives no benefit from
holding it, other than its fee for acting as share trustee. The trust will terminate only after the Notes and
all other obligations of the Issuer under the Transaction Documents have been discharged in full.

The purpose of the share trust is that neither the Issuer nor Holdings is a subsidiary undertaking of the
Society or of any other party to the Transaction, so that their assets and liabilities are not consolidated with
those of the Society and are not affected by the insolvency of the Society. Neither the Society nor any other
party to the Transaction controls, directly or indirectly, the Issuer, Holdings or the Share Trustee.

### Employees and Subsidiaries

Neither the Issuer nor Holdings has any employees. The day-to-day administration of the Issuer is carried out by
the Corporate Services Provider; the Mortgage Loans are administered by the Mortgage Administrator; and the
Issuer Accounts are operated by the Cash Manager, in each case under the relevant Transaction Document. The
Issuer has no subsidiaries, and Holdings has no subsidiary other than the Issuer.

### Accounts and Auditors

The accounting reference date of each of the Issuer and Holdings is 31 December. The first financial period of
the Issuer will run from its incorporation to 31 December 2026, and its first audited accounts will be filed at
Companies House within six months after that date, as required of a public company by the Companies Act 2006.
The first financial period of Holdings will end on the same date, and its accounts will be filed within nine
months after it. Each will prepare its accounts in accordance with United Kingdom generally accepted accounting
practice, including Financial Reporting Standard 102. No financial statements of the Issuer or of Holdings have
been prepared or published as at the date of this Offering Circular.

Fennick & Rowe LLP, chartered accountants and registered auditors, of London, have been appointed as auditors of
the Issuer and of Holdings. Fennick & Rowe LLP is registered to carry out audit work in the United Kingdom by the
Institute of Chartered Accountants in England and Wales and has no material interest in the Issuer or Holdings.
Copies of the audited accounts of the Issuer, when published, will be available as described in Section 20 and
will be delivered to the Note Trustee.
